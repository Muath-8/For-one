What runs where

Four deployables and two stores inside one AWS region, every vendor behind an adapter. Live roles read the learner model; only the after-session pass writes it. Inngest carries ids, never content.

One Docker image, three entrypoints. forone-python runs api, workflows and call-worker selected by one environment variable; the image bakes in ffmpeg, spaCy, the LiveKit turn detector and Silero VAD so a worker cold-starts inside 60 s.

03

The episode factory and what each station costs

One Inngest function, idempotent per episode, with cut lines at drop − 4 h (teacher), − 2 h 30 (writer) and − 90 min (switch to on-demand pricing). Bars are cost per daily episode at list prices; voice is the line to attack.

Coverage is code. The fitter lemmatizes the script, scores every running unit with the learner's Beta mean (or the frequency prior from the 48-item word check), and fails any sentence holding an unplanned unit below p = 0.5. Prompts, schemas and the algorithm are in 03-generation-pipeline.md.

04

The live call: one second per turn, and only recasts

The host declares its moves in a header line before it speaks; a governor in code refuses any move outside the budget. Streaming recognizers smooth learner errors, so every turn is re-transcribed verbatim afterwards.

recast

At most one per learner turn, only on the session's grammar target or an error that blurred meaning, the corrected word stressed inside a reply with its own point. The caption brightens the span for one second with no label. Never in the first call.

clarify · nudge · gap

Three clarification requests, one nudge, two to four recall gaps and one 60 to 90 s retell per main call; anxiety mode (short turns or two 8 s silences) drops the nudge and keeps one gap.

verbatim

Each turn's audio is encoded, stored under a per-call key and transcribed with disfluencies by a second recognizer; the analyst reads only that text. With voice storage off the audio is deleted the moment the transcript lands, and in any case within 15 minutes.

never

"You said X, it should be Y", grammar names, praise for grammar, scores. The governor logs a violation, re-requests once, and the offline suite fails the build on a scripted call that produces any of them.

Memory you can open, claims that passed a gate

Nine stores are derived from the log and rebuildable. A life fact exists only with the learner's exact words and the event they came from. A progress sentence exists only as a claim row written by code after five checks.

The claim gate, in the order the checks run
4 · clean evidence

Verbatim done, recognizer confidence over the span at or above the package threshold, no dial-in or voice-note source.

5 · openable

The quote is in the log and an audio range or a text fallback exists, so "how we know" is a render of the row.

1 · like for like

Same task type, same assessment version; a then-and-now compares different stimulus forms.

2 · beyond noise

A standard error from at least 12 test-retest pairs exists, and the change is at least twice it. Until then only did-it, first-ever and word-owned claims can pass.

3 · repeated

The two most recent comparable segments point the same way, each beyond the threshold.

What the decision records settled, and what is still owed

The architecture was revised on 6 November with 23 decision records. Three of this design's first stances were overridden and are now corrected; the rest of the list, 21 items, is in the open-questions file.

1 · notes · settled

After-call notes, the polish card and the margin note do not exist (ADR-06). The after-session pass is one function inside 10 minutes; corrections are recasts planned by the teacher's echo plan; Salma explains only when asked; a "New for you" card comes only from passed claims.

2 · languages · settled

Two package axes, not pairs (ADR-02). packages/languages/<target>/ holds voices, recognizers, lexicon and inventory; packages/native/<lang>/ holds interface strings, consent, Salma and crisis resources. The pilot runs on languages/en and native/ar; a target opens by the five-check gate in code.

3 · plans · settled

Plans one_a_day, two_a_day, three_a_day (ADR-04, ADR-05). Episodes 2 and 3 are daily_extra: planned overnight with the drop, made on demand through POST /v1/episodes/next after the current episode is heard, never autoplayed. Still open: whether an extra gets its own call.

13 · vendors

Voice and recognizer vendors are open until the blind test, the 30 clips and the 50 calls run in week 1. Defaults: Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS, Soniox, AssemblyAI. Hume is excluded.

15 · bibles

Nobody has written Karim, Mona and Salma. Week 2 cannot test real voices without them; due before 18 November.

12 · counsel

Home region defaults to eu-central-1 (ADR-08), me-central-1 the alternative. Counsel's answers on region, transfer basis, regimes covered and crisis lines are due 17 November, before build week 1.
