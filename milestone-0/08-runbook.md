# 8. The daily runbook

*Milestone 0 pilot kit. One page to follow every day from Sunday 1 to Monday 16 November. Times are Muath's (Saudi time); each learner's slot is written in their own time in their learner file. Details behind each step: [04-production.md](04-production.md).*

## Morning (from 06:15, about 30 minutes)

1. **Open WhatsApp.** Read overnight messages. In the tracker, mark any unprompted asks, reactions that came in late, and anything anyone said about the show (Notes column, their words).
2. **Anything urgent?** A complaint, a wrong memory, someone wanting to stop, anything worrying: answer it first, as yourself, kindly.
3. **Check today's drops are ready.** Each learner with a new episode has: the MP3 in `episodes/`, the article, the actions message (call link and writing question), and their Mona agent shows tonight's brief. Anyone missing: finish them before their slot, or send the one-line "a day late" message.
4. **06:30 slot.** Send the three drop messages to each learner in this slot: audio, article, actions. Do not send to anyone on their day off, or to anyone who has not touched the last episode.
5. **Set reminders** for the 12:30, 17:30 and 21:00 slots.

## During the day (5 minutes per slot)

- **12:30, 17:30, 21:00:** send that slot's drops.
- **Answer messages** as they come. Note every unprompted ask. Do not chase anyone: no reminders, no "did you listen?".
- If a learner asks for a word or how something works, answer briefly as yourself.

## Evening (from 20:00; about 25 minutes per learner who had a session, less with batching)

Work step by step across all learners, not learner by learner.

| | Step | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Collect.** Download each call's audio; Scribe v2; copy written replies, voice notes and any question they asked; fill today's Daily row (listened, reaction, called, minutes, wrote, unprompted ask). | Every learner has a Daily row, including those with nothing today |
| 2 | **Analyse.** M0 Analyst chat per learner with a session: learner file, today's brief, transcript, written reply, next episode type. | Each has an analysis |
| 3 | **Send Salma's message** if there is one (the 3 ideas before a personal episode, the one-liner before a generic one, answers to their questions). Nothing about their English. Check the errors to echo against the recording. | Sent before 21:30 |
| 4 | **Update the learner file** (echo list too); confirm or drop proposed facts; copy MEASURES into Daily and ECHO CHECK into Echoes. | Files current |
| 5 | **Write** (M0 Writer) for every learner who gets a new episode tomorrow. Generic and return episodes first; personal ones once the pick is in, or after 22:00 with the recommended idea. | Packages written |
| 6 | **Coverage check**: script 95%+, article 98%+; rewrite flagged sentences. | All pass |
| 7 | **Editor** (Gemini): fix everything it flags. | SHIP |
| 8 | **Read it yourself.** True callbacks only. Nothing that feels like a correction. Would they pick this over a real podcast? | You'd send it to a friend |
| 9 | **Voice** it; listen to the opening and one random minute. | MP3s saved |
| 10 | **Load** each call brief into the learner's Mona agent. | Agents updated |
| 11 | **Queue** tomorrow's drops by slot; log production minutes. | Done |

## Rules that never bend

- No streaks, scores, levels, percentages or "you missed a day" anywhere, from anyone.
- Nobody corrects. No notes on anyone's English, no quoted mistakes, no "the right way is". Explanations only when the learner asks.
- Nothing new goes to a learner who has not touched the last episode; after 3+ days, the next one is a return episode.
- A callback uses only a CONFIRMED fact. When in doubt, leave it out.
- Never call while driving: if a learner says they are driving, they call later.
- Distress or self-harm in a call or message: read it tonight, reply personally as yourself, point to help (their local emergency number, from the learner file), and keep the show off that topic.
- Change the design only to fix something broken or harmful, and log it in the Changes tab.
- Save every chat, transcript and note. They are the beta's first test set.

## Weekly (Fridays)

- Copy Claude, AI Studio and ElevenLabs usage into the Costs tab.
- Check ElevenLabs call minutes left on the plan.
- Look at production minutes per learner: which step is slowest? That is the first thing the beta automates.
