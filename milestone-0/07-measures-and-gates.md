# 7. What to measure, and the pass and stop numbers

*Milestone 0 pilot kit. Updated 23 October 2026: monthly plans in the pre-pay ask, a new gate 8 for echoed errors, and a check-in question on feeling corrected in place of the old question on Salma's notes. The pass numbers are concept v2's Milestone 0 gate (section 9), which merges the business, enjoyment, personalization and assessment lenses. The stop lines are not in any lens for Milestone 0; they are this kit's judgment, set at roughly the beta's stop ratios, and marked as such. Every measure has a column in [pilot-tracker.xlsx](pilot-tracker.xlsx), and the **Gates** tab computes them.*

If a gate fails, find which claim failed before writing code: **the show is not wanted** (asks, sessions, worth it, choosing tomorrow), **the teacher is not felt** (speech minutes, then-and-now, echoed errors), or **the price is wrong** (pre-pay). Personal versus generic decides how deep personalization should go (decision 8), not whether to build.

## Every day, per learner (the **Daily** tab)

Fill these in during the evening collect step; one row per learner per day, including days off.

| Column | How to record it |
|---|---|
| Episode sent | Episode number, or blank if none was sent |
| Type | PILOT, PERSONAL, GENERIC, RETURN or FINALE |
| Listened | 1 if the learner reacted to the episode audio or mentioned it |
| Reaction | ❤️, 👍 or 😐 (learners are asked to react: ❤️ loved it, 👍 good, 😐 meh). Blank if none. |
| Called | 1 if a call of 2+ minutes happened |
| Call minutes | From the agent history |
| Learner words | From the analyst's MEASURES |
| Own speech minutes | From the analyst's MEASURES |
| Wrote | 1 if they answered the writing question (or sent a voice note instead of the call) |
| Session | Formula: listened and (called or wrote) |
| Choice offered / chosen | 1 if the 3 ideas were sent tonight; 1 if the learner picked one (or proposed their own) before production |
| Unprompted ask | 1 if, without being asked, the learner asked for an episode: on a day off, when it was late, after a gap, or after the finale |
| Wrong memory | 1 if the learner said a callback was wrong |
| Minutes to produce | Muath's time on this learner tonight (rough) |
| Notes | Anything said about the show, good or bad, in their words |

**Echoes:** every evening, copy the analyst's ECHO CHECK into the **Echoes** tab, one row per item: learner, day number, item, the day it was first echoed, times needed today, times wrong today. Log an item only in sessions after the day it was first echoed. The tab works out the rest.

**Costs:** once a week, write the totals from the Claude, AI Studio billing and ElevenLabs usage pages into the **Costs** tab. Divided by sessions, it gives the first real cost per session (concept v2 budget for the beta: $0.60; stop line $1.00). Hand-made costs are not the product's costs, but the call and voice minutes are close to real.

## The side-by-side with a human-made show

Concept v2 asks for one comparison against a human-made learner podcast. Once, around day 9 or 10: pick an episode of BBC Learning English's *6 Minute English* on a topic close to the learner's interests, and make that learner's next personal episode on the same topic. Send the BBC episode as a link (do not re-upload their audio) next to the For one episode, and ask: "Tomorrow morning you have 7 minutes. Which one would you rather listen to, and why?" Record the answer in the **Daily** tab's notes and the **Gates** tab. This is side by side, not blind (the BBC show is recognizable); say so when reporting it.

## The pre-pay ask

On day 15, with the week-3 recording task, a separate message. The offer is concept v2's (decision 2): a monthly subscription in three plans by new episodes a day, 1 a day $19.99, **2 a day $29.99 (recommended)**, 3 a day $44.99, and the first 100 paying learners keep their plan's price for as long as they stay subscribed. The pilot asks for the first month now; that month starts when the founding beta opens on 23 December. Pre-payers skip the beta's 7-day trial, because the 7-day refund does the same job.

The pricing psychology is concept v2's and stays honest: three plans with the middle one marked "recommended", the top plan as the anchor, the price per day for the recommended plan, the real comparison with a tutor, and no fake countdowns or fake scarcity (the 100 is a real limit).

## The gates

Concept v2's Milestone 0 gate, every line of which must pass to start the beta build on 18 November. N is the number of learners who started (15 if nobody dropped before day 3).

| # | Gate | Pass (concept v2) | Stop and rethink (this kit's judgment) | Claim it tests | Tracker formula |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Learners who asked for the next episode unprompted, at least once | 10 of 15 | 5 or fewer | The show is wanted | Count of learners with any unprompted ask |
| 2 | Sessions a week (each learner's sessions ÷ 2, then the median across learners) | median 4+ | under 2.5 | The show earns the time | Median of per-learner sessions per week |
| 3 | Own speech minutes per session (each learner's median, then the median across learners) | 4+ | under 2 | The teacher gets output | Median of per-learner medians |
| 4a | In week 2, still choosing tomorrow (learners who chose on at least half of the week-2 choices offered) | most (8+ of 15) | 4 or fewer | Pull lasts past novelty | Count |
| 4b | "Worth it" on the day-14 check-in ("نعم") | most (8+ of 15) | 4 or fewer | Liking, not just wanting | Count |
| 5 | Improved in the blind then-and-now ([06-then-and-now.md](06-then-and-now.md#the-gate-for-claims-milestone-0-version-of-the-claim-gate)) | most of those with both recordings | under a third, or no measurable change for anyone | The teacher makes it count | Count of "improved" in Ratings |
| 6 | Personal days beat generic days | personal wins for more than half the learners on reactions, and personal sessions have a higher call rate or more speech minutes overall | generic equal or better | Personalization depth (decision 8) | Per-learner and pooled comparison |
| 7 | Pre-paid a first month, any plan | 5 of 15 | 1 or none | The price | Count of payments |
| 8 | The errors the show echoed back appear less often by day 14 (each learner's error rate on echoed items, week 2 against week 1, counting only items first echoed by day 7 and learners with at least 3 chances in each week) | the rate falls for more than half of those learners (this kit's reading of concept v2's line) | falls for under a third | Invisible teaching works | Echoes tab; count of learners whose rate fell |

**Guardrails** (not gates, but each one changes the beta design if it fails):

| Guardrail | Line | If it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Wrong memories | at most 1 in the whole pilot (concept v2's beta line is under 1 in 100 callbacks) | Tighten the "confirmed facts only" rule before building memory |
| Regret ("إيه" on either check-in) | at most 2 learners | The pull is the unhealthy kind; review episode length and call cap |
| Felt corrected or tested "often" (either check-in) | at most 2 learners | The recasts or recall gaps are too visible; soften them before the beta |
| "Very disappointed" if it disappeared | track; the beta target is 40%+ | Below 25% is the beta's stop line; treat it as an early warning here |
| Side-by-side with the BBC show | at least half choose For one | If fewer, the 8am test fails: the writing, not the plumbing, is the problem |
| Production minutes per learner | track | Shows which manual step to automate first in the beta |
| Learners who stopped | track with their reason | Read every reason before deciding anything |

## Reading the results on 18 November

- **Gates 1, 2, 4 pass, 3 or 5 fail:** the show is wanted, the teaching is not yet working. Change the call (more of it, the observer, stronger recasts) before the build, not the show.
- **Gates 3 and 5 pass, 1, 2 or 4 fail:** learners improve but do not come back. Look at the reactions by episode type and the week-1 versus week-2 drop; the variety system and the cue matter most.
- **Gate 8 fails while gate 3 passes:** learners talk, but the echoes do not land. Try more echoes per episode and recasts on more errors before anything else. Whether invisible teaching needs an explicit mode that learners can ask for is Muath's call (decision 3), and the number of learners who asked for corrections (Changes tab) is the evidence.
- **Gate 6 fails:** personalization is decoration for these learners (personalization lens). Build interest-led episodes at the level, keep life facts thin, and save the cost.
- **Gate 7 fails but the others pass:** the product works and the price or the offer is wrong. Test the price (decision 2) before building payments.
- **Most gates fail:** revise the thesis, not the backlog (PRD Part 18). Read every learner's notes and the reasons people stopped first.
