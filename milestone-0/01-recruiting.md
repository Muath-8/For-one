# 1. Recruiting and screening

*Milestone 0 pilot kit. Updated 23 October 2026 for Muath's decisions: no launch country, as many languages as the technology allows, invisible teaching. Goal: 15 learners start on Sunday 1 or Monday 2 November (the day after their intake call), plus 3 reserves. Recruit about 25 applicants to get there.*

## Who we want

Concept v2, decision 1: anyone, anywhere, with no target country or group of people. The pilot recruits through Muath's own network, so most applicants will come from wherever that network is, and that is fine. The only limits are the ones a hand-made pilot needs.

| Must have | Why |
|---|---|
| 18 or older (any adult age; 22 to 40 is where most applicants will be) | Under 18 is excluded outright. |
| Any country, any first language except English | No launch country (decision 1). |
| Learning **English**, at about B1: understands a lot, freezes or stalls when speaking | Every episode is checked by hand, so the target language must be one Muath can judge (concept v2, Milestone 0 row). The show format needs about 2,000+ known words. Another target language is possible only with a native-speaker helper (see below). |
| A real reason, with a date if possible (interview, new job, study, a move, a trip) | Seasons need a real goal; a dated goal gives the arc. |
| Can give 20 to 30 minutes on at least 4 days a week, 1 to 16 November | The gate measures 4+ sessions a week. |
| Can take a 5-minute call somewhere quiet with earphones, never while driving | The call is where speaking is measured. |
| Uses WhatsApp daily | Delivery channel. |
| Lives where at least one drop slot falls inside Muath's day | Episodes are sent by hand. The slots are 06:30, 12:30, 17:30 and 21:00 Saudi time; convert them to the learner's time and offer the ones that work. Learners in the Americas will usually get the 17:30 or 21:00 slot as their morning. |

**Exclude:** English teachers, people who already speak comfortably (C1 and up), people who cannot yet form a sentence in English, more than 5 of Muath's close friends (they will be too kind; friends of friends are better).

**Balance (an aim, not a rule):** at least 3 countries, at least 2 first languages, no more than 10 learners from one country, and a mix of men and women. This tests "not overfitting" in a small way. If the network cannot reach that mix, run anyway and say so in the gate report.

**Language of the show's messages.** The hosts always speak English. The show's own messages (the evening choice, check-ins, the then-and-now message) go out in the language the learner picks: Arabic or English, written by Muath, or another language, translated by the M0 Analyst from the English versions in this kit. Tell learners who pick a third language that those messages are machine-translated and unchecked.

For applicants who would rather not do a live intake call with Muath, the intake works by voice notes too (see [04-production.md](04-production.md#intake)).

## Another target language (optional)

Concept v2 allows one more target language in the pilot if Muath has a native-speaker helper who can read every script, check the hosts' language and judge the recordings. Without that helper, everyone learns English. If he does have one, give that language at most 3 of the 15 places, and know that the word check, the coverage checker, the then-and-now forms and the voice tests in this kit are English-only and would need the helper's versions first. The kit's recommendation is English only for this pilot. The language gate in concept v2 (section 8) runs separately, in parallel.

## Where to post (Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October)

In this order, from the business lens (section 6), now without a country focus:

1. **Muath's own story**, in Arabic and in English, on his WhatsApp status, X, LinkedIn, Snapchat or Instagram: one post, the message below.
2. **Direct messages** to 20 to 30 people in his network, in any country, asking them to forward it to one friend who fits ("who do you know who understands English but freezes when they talk?"). Ask people abroad in particular: they are the cheapest way to get past one country.
3. **WhatsApp and Telegram groups**: university alumni groups, workplace groups, IELTS and English-practice groups he is already in.
4. **LinkedIn**, the English version, aimed at young professionals preparing for interviews anywhere.

No paid ads.

## The screening form (Google Form, English with Arabic)

Make it in Google Forms on Saturday; responses go to a Google Sheet. Write each question in English and put the Arabic in the question's description, so one form serves everyone. Both are below.

**Section 1. About you (عنك)**

1. First name (الاسم الأول) — short answer
2. WhatsApp number, with country code (رقم الواتساب مع رمز الدولة) — short answer
3. Age (العمر) — multiple choice: under 18 / 18–21 / 22–28 / 29–35 / 36–45 / 46 and over. *Under 18 ends the form with a polite thank-you (use "Go to section based on answer").*
4. Country and city where you live (البلد والمدينة) — short answer
5. Your first language (لغتك الأولى) — short answer
6. Which language should the show's messages to you be in? The episodes are always in English. (بأي لغة تريد رسائل البرنامج؟ الحلقات دائماً بالإنجليزية) — Arabic / English / Other: ___
7. The hosts will talk about you in English. How should they refer to you? (المقدّمون سيتكلمون عنك بالإنجليزية، كيف يشيرون إليك؟) — he / she

**Section 2. Your English (الإنجليزية عندك)**

8. Years of English study (كم سنة درست الإنجليزية؟) — under 2 / 2–5 / 6–10 / more than 10
9. When you hear a podcast or video in English about a topic you know: (عندما تسمع بودكاست أو فيديو بالإنجليزية عن موضوع تعرفه)
   - I understand only a few words / I understand part of it / I understand most of it / I understand it easily
   - (أفهم كلمات قليلة فقط / أفهم جزءاً / أفهم أغلبه / أفهمه بسهولة)
10. When you speak English: (عندما تتكلم الإنجليزية)
   - I can't form sentences / I speak slowly with many pauses / I can talk about topics I know but get stuck looking for words / I speak comfortably
   - (لا أستطيع تكوين جمل / أتكلم ببطء مع وقفات كثيرة / أتكلم عن مواضيع أعرفها لكن أتوقف وأبحث عن الكلمات / أتكلم براحة)
11. **Word check (about 2 minutes).** Checkbox question, "Shuffle option order" on. Instruction text:
   *"Tick the words whose meaning you know. Some are not real words; don't tick those. No score, no pass or fail; it tells the show where to start."*
   *"علّم على الكلمات التي تعرف معناها. بعض الكلمات ليست حقيقية، لا تعلّم عليها. لا توجد درجة ولا نجاح أو رسوب؛ فقط لنعرف من أين نبدأ الحلقات."*
   Options, all 65 (50 real words in 10 frequency bands, 15 invented words):
   ```
   daughter, against, become, blood, above,
   effort, reaction, memory, direction, correct,
   profit, assume, represent, severe, pleasure,
   decent, priority, obtain, suffer, finger,
   overseas, stability, lazy, scope, ruin,
   yield, adopt, retain, gravity, theft,
   warn, concentrate, cease, fridge, continental,
   relieved, expose, siege, theoretical, decorated,
   carved, prosperity, intersection, thrust, liable,
   imply, specify, stimulus, willingness, hawk,
   plindle, crandish, flemmon, porlick, treavy,
   brasper, gondify, drosting, mollick, prandle,
   fendle, turvish, glimmock, spantle, plorrow
   ```
   Each row of five real words is one band of 1,000 words by frequency (row 1 = words ranked 1 to 1,000 in English, row 10 = 9,001 to 10,000; ranks from the wordfreq English list, checked on 2026-10-10). The last three rows are invented. Scoring is automatic in the **Screening** tab of [pilot-tracker.xlsx](pilot-tracker.xlsx): paste the cell from the responses sheet and it estimates vocabulary size. The word list works for any first language; speakers of languages close to English (Spanish, French, German and others) may know some rarer words through shared roots, so trust the voice note more for them.

**Section 3. Your goal and your day (هدفك ويومك)**

12. Why do you want to improve your English, and is there a date? For example "interview in December" (لماذا تريد تطوير إنجليزيتك؟ وهل هناك موعد معيّن؟) — paragraph
13. What could you talk about for an hour without getting bored? (ما الموضوع الذي تستطيع أن تتكلم عنه ساعة كاملة دون ملل؟) — paragraph
14. When in your day could you listen for 7 minutes, in your local time? (متى في يومك تستطيع أن تسمع ٧ دقائق، بتوقيتك المحلي؟) — early morning / on the way to work / lunch / after work / evening
15. Can you commit to at least 4 days a week from 1 to 16 November? (هل تستطيع الالتزام ٤ أيام في الأسبوع على الأقل من ١ إلى ١٦ نوفمبر؟) — yes / not sure / no
16. Can you take a 5-minute call somewhere quiet with earphones, not while driving? (هل تستطيع إجراء مكالمة ٥ دقائق في مكان هادئ مع سماعات، وليس أثناء القيادة؟) — yes / sometimes / no
17. Do you know Muath? (هل تعرف معاذ شخصياً؟) — close friend / acquaintance / no

**Section 4. Last step (آخر خطوة)**

18. Text only, no question: *"Send a voice note in English, about 45 seconds, to [WhatsApp number]: tell us about your last weekend. Mistakes and pauses are fine; that is exactly what we want to hear."*
   *"أرسل رسالة صوتية بالإنجليزية، ٤٥ ثانية تقريباً، إلى الرقم [رقم الواتساب]: احكِ لنا عن آخر عطلة نهاية أسبوع لك. لا بأس بالأخطاء أو الوقفات؛ هذا بالضبط ما نريد أن نسمعه."*

## Choosing the 15 (score as applications arrive; final pick Thursday 29 October)

Score each applicant in the **Screening** tab. Then:

| Check | Keep if |
|---|---|
| Age, first language, time zone | 18+; first language is not English; at least one drop slot falls in their day |
| Commitment (Q15, Q16) | "Yes" to both, or "yes" and "sometimes" |
| Word check estimate | about 2,000 to 6,500 words (the tab flags outside this range) |
| False alarms | 3 or fewer invented words ticked (more means the estimate is unreliable; judge by the voice note) |
| Speaking can-do (Q10) | options 2 or 3 |
| Voice note | Keeps going for 30+ seconds in sentences, with errors and pauses. Not fluent, not single words. |
| Goal (Q12) | A real reason; a date is a plus |

Pick 15 who pass, preferring a dated goal, the balance above, and no more than 5 close friends. Keep 3 more as reserves: a reserve starts if someone drops before day 3. Everyone else goes on the waitlist for the beta.

Give each learner an ID, **L01 to L15** (reserves R1 to R3), in the order you accept them. Odd IDs start the personal/generic alternation on a personal episode, even IDs on a generic one (see [04-production.md](04-production.md#personal-and-generic-days)). Write each learner's country, first language, message language and local emergency number in the **Roster** tab and the learner file; the call host and the consent form need the emergency number.
