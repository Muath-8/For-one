What was cut

The original plan was an architecture phase, then a design phase. The run stopped on the usage limit and you asked to finish up, so this set is what exists.

  • The whole design phase. Eleven designers, their reviews and the clickable prototype never ran. design/ is empty.
  • Seven of eighteen reviews. 07, 08 and 09 have no review; 06 has the conformance review but no skeptic review.
  • Revisions for 03 to 06. Their review findings are listed on each page but not applied to the text.
  • The integration pass. No one has reconciled the nine documents against each other after the reviews. Event names and a few interfaces disagree between documents; the reviews flag several.
