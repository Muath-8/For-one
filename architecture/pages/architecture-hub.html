What changed on 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-12

The 2026-10-23 run stopped on the usage limit with 07 to 09 unreviewed and 03 to 06 unrevised. This pass finished the set.

  • Six new reviews. 07, 08 and 09 were each read by the conformance and the skeptic lens: 60 findings, six blockers, all applied.
  • Every document revised. The 2026-10-23 decisions had moved under the documents: no launch country, USD monthly plans with 1, 2 or 3 episodes a day, no correction notes, a build re-dated to 2026-11-18. Document 10 records the 22 rulings and each document's section 11 says what changed.
  • The integration pass. Names that disagreed between documents (`episodes.state`, `call_turn_verbatim`, `word_uses`, `expects_drop`, package paths) have one spelling now, listed in document 10 section 4 for the technical design.
  • 2026-11-12: every open item decided (ADR-24). About 150 OPEN tags across the documents became decisions, owner actions or measurements; 33 consistency rulings settled disagreements between documents; the skeptic review of 06 ran and was applied; the backlog was re-cut.
  • 2026-11-12, later: ADR-25. An AI agent builds it; no calendar dates; no lawyer; web only; no pilot and no beta.
  • Drafts. Content drafts (bibles) are in content/; the self-reviewed legal texts are in legal/ (ADR-25.3).
