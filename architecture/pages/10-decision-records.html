The decisions

Section 1's table, one card per decision. "Rejected" is what was considered and turned down.

  1. ADR-01

    Source of truth for the architecture

    decisions.md (2026-10-23) over concept-v2 over PRD-v2; the nine documents are revised to it; a PRD-v3 carries the same changes [DECISION]

    Why The architecture must build what Muath decided, not what the PRD said two weeks earlier

    Rejected Waiting for PRD-v3 before revising (the technical design depends on these documents now)

  2. ADR-02

    Languages as two package kinds

    A target-language package packages/languages/<target>/ (voices, recognizers, lexicon, structure inventory, calibration set, law words, crisis keywords, prompts) and a native-language package packages/native/<lang>/ (interface strings, consent texts, privacy notice, gloss language, Salma's voice and lines, crisis clips and resources); package_ar_en is split into lang/en and native/ar; every learner carries native_language, target_language, residence_country, tz [DECISION, decisions.md §1, concept-v2 §7 and §8]

    Why No launch pair means the pair is a per-learner fact; the two sides have different bars (the gate for a target, strings for a native language)

    Rejected One package per pair (n × m packages); hard-coding English and Arabic until "later"

  3. ADR-03

    The language gate as code

    forone.langgate runs concept-v2 §8's five checks per target language from vendor-lab; a target opens when its gate run passes; English is the pilot target and the only one that must pass before the beta; the gate runs in parallel with Milestone 0 [DECISION]

    Why 08's vendor tests and 09's recognizer suite are the gate's first three checks; one harness, one evidence folder

    Rejected Vendor tests as one-off scripts per language

  4. ADR-04

    Plans and allowance

    Three monthly USD plans: one_a_day $19.99, two_a_day $29.99 (recommended, the trial plan), three_a_day $44.99; the allowance is plan.episodes_per_day new episodes per learner-local day; one swap a day stays; listening, the article, the chat and round two are unlimited; founding = the first 100 payers keep their plan's price (founding_locked), no yearly plan [DECISION, decisions.md §2]

    Why Decided by Muath; the cost model sets the price

    Rejected SAR founding year and yearly plan (PRD-v2 1.4)

  5. ADR-05

    How episodes 2 and 3 are made

    The first new episode of the day is produced by the batch pipeline for the drop; episodes 2 and 3 are made on request ("next one", a learner action, never autoplay) through the on-demand path, streamed under 30 s, with the teacher's plan for them prepared in the nightly run so no Opus call sits on the path; the nightly run may pre-produce episode 2 for learners whose last 7 days show they use it [DECISION, concept-v2 law 11]

    Why Typical use is 50 to 60% of the allowance, so pre-producing everything wastes about a third of the voice line; the on-demand path already exists

    Rejected Pre-producing the full allowance; a "next episode" feed

  6. ADR-06

    Invisible teaching in the architecture

    Salma's after-call notes, the polish card and the chat margin note are removed; session/after-pass loses stage 1 (the 90-second notes step) and keeps the 10-minute pass; the correction guard (07) blocks explicit corrections from every host and Salma line in code; the editor's check "nothing reads as a correction" is a model check; "say it once" and shadowing stay as pronunciation; the notebook, the weekly proof, "why this episode" and "where do I stand" stay as evidence [DECISION, decisions.md §3 and §4, concept-v2 law 7]

    Why Muath's call; law 7 must be enforced by code the way law 12 is

    Rejected Keeping notes as an option behind a flag (a flag never changes a law, 09 D8)

  7. ADR-07

    Dates

    Milestone 0 2026-10-24 to 11-18; language gate and vendor tests during it; build 2026-11-18 to 12-22 in five weeks; beta 2026-12-23 to 2027-01-26; gate review 2027-01-26; every "2026-11-02" in the first drafts moves 16 days [DECISION, milestone-0/03-schedule.md]

    Why The pilot had not started when the first drafts were written

    Rejected Keeping the old dates "until the PRD moves"

  8. ADR-08

    Home region

    Default HOME_REGION = eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) for a worldwide cohort; me-central-1 (UAE) remains a one-day config move if counsel and the cohort favour it; call-worker runs where the vendors are fastest; final answer stays [OPEN for Muath, with counsel] [DECISION on the default only]

    Why With no launch country, EU and UK learners' data in the UAE is itself a transfer without adequacy; an EU region needs only the AWS DPA for them

    Rejected GCP Dammam now; a region per learner (two of everything)

  9. ADR-09

    Tracing data (Langfuse)

    01 D15 stands for every domain: metadata only by default (ids, versions, usage, cost, latency, prompt hash); prompt and completion text only for learners with data_use = true, after anonymize(); user_id is the research pseudonym, never the learner id; 30-day retention; debugging reads prompts from the home region (02's pipeline_steps artifacts, the call brief and turns in events) [DECISION]

    Why Three documents had three rules for one processor; the strictest one is also the one 01 already designed

    Rejected 08 D3 (full text, learner id, 100%)

  10. ADR-10

    Embeddings vendor

    Google's embedding model under the existing Google processor row; no OpenAI processor [DECISION, 02 §9.7]

    Why One fewer processor for one cosine check

    Rejected OpenAI text-embedding-3-small (07 and 08 first drafts)

  11. ADR-11

    Editor fallback

    When the second vendor is down the editor runs on claude-opus-5-5 (standard), flagged same_vendor_edit for review; never code checks only [DECISION, 02 §9.7]

    Why The editor must be at least as strong as the writer when it shares the writer's vendor

    Rejected Haiku as the fallback editor (08 first draft)

  12. ADR-12

    Media key model

    Learner-voice kinds (call_turn, call_full, recording, voice_note, say_it_once, shadow, clip, the name clip) are SSE-C under the learner's DEK; produced show kinds (episode_segment, episode_full, episode_feed, read_aloud, recap, feed_tag, proof) are SSE-KMS under the environment key, served by short-lived presigned URLs, deleted by prefix in learner/delete [DECISION, amends 07 D1 and D3, 01 D7]

    Why SSE-C can never be served by a presigned URL, a CDN or <audio src>; locking show audio behind api bandwidth forever was an accident of the first draft

    Rejected Everything SSE-C (07 D1); everything SSE-KMS (loses "key deletion deletes the voice")

  13. ADR-13

    Cost rows and the spend counter

    vendor_calls rows are written in their own short transaction the moment a vendor responds, idempotent on (inngest_run_id, step, attempt) or (call_id, turn_index, capability); the same statement upserts learner_spend_daily; the worker appends each turn's rows to an S3 spool before any buffered flush; reconciliation hourly [DECISION, amends 08 §3.2 and 07 §4.11]

    Why A cost row inside the caller's transaction is lost on exactly the retry the spend guard exists to catch

    Rejected Same-transaction writes; nightly reconciliation

  14. ADR-14

    Spend modes and model levers

    Automatic spend modes may change only what no learner hears (force batch, cap rewrites, skip canaries and the nightly smoke, defer anonymization) and may pause on-demand extras under the daily cap; model downgrades (Haiku host, Sonnet teacher) are feature flags a person flips after eval-gate passes [DECISION, 09 D8 over 08 D4 conserve]

    Why A quality change is a human decision, not an alarm's

    Rejected 08's automatic conserve downgrades

  15. ADR-15

    Per-plan cost guard and caps

    Soft guard 2.5 × episodes_per_day × budget, hard 4 × episodes × budget; call starts 6 × episodes a day; ideas 3 per episode slot; daily spend cap Σ episodes × $1.00 over learners; the weekly report gains "Margin by plan", plan_mix and blended_margin [DECISION, amends 07 D11 and 08]

    Why Every per-day limit in the first drafts assumed one episode a day

    Rejected Fixed $2.50 / $5.00 guards

  16. ADR-16

    Operations around the clock

    No protected hour and no timezone-fixed job: every rule is relative to each learner's drop_at; measures/daily and privacy/verify run hourly over learner-local days that closed; reports at fixed UTC times; the release tool computes the deploy window from learner_schedule (deploy only when under 2% of learners have a drop in the next 45 min, call-worker only when idle) and the check is binding; per-learner lateness is P2, systemic lateness (3+ learners or 5%) is P1; staging stays up from build week 2 through the beta [DECISION, amends 09 D9, D11, 4.10 and 01 D14]

    Why Learners anywhere means drops at every hour

    Rejected A Riyadh-centred rota and release window

  17. ADR-17

    Database roles for staff surfaces

    forone_admin serves the /admin pages (aggregate reads across learners, writes only to the admin tables), with every content read in audit_log; pgaudit applies only to forone_human, the role psql sessions use [DECISION, amends 07 §4.2 and 09 D2]

    Why pgaudit on the dashboards' role would bury the human-SQL audit it exists for

    Rejected Dashboards on forone_readonly under pgaudit

  18. ADR-18

    DEK cache and audit

    keys.get_dek caches 60 s in api and workflows, never for admin_break_glass or export; one audit_log row per (learner_id, purpose, actor) per cache window with a uses count; cache entries are keyed by (learner_id, key_version) and destroy() bumps the version in the row so every process misses on its next read; the zeroization claim is dropped [DECISION, amends 07 D2 and §4.1]

    Why A 10-minute cache made the audit log incomplete and let a destroyed key keep working in other tasks

    Rejected Per-read KMS calls (latency and cost); the first draft's cache

  19. ADR-19

    Naming rulings

    episodes.state (not status); events append-only with call_turn_verbatim as a second event appended by workflows through events.append(); word_uses is 04's per-use word evidence table written by 05 (the twin of structure_observations); learner_schedule.expects_drop; tools/call-bench is the one load and latency tool; cohort_key = invite_date; proofs.sessions_in_week is computed, not stored [DECISION]

    Why Nine documents, one vocabulary; the technical design needs the names fixed

    Rejected Leaving each document's spelling

  20. ADR-20

    Jurisdiction and crisis resources

    learners.residence_country is asked at A9, never inferred; 07 §4.9 becomes a regime table (EU and UK GDPR, Saudi PDPL, a generic baseline for unreviewed countries) with the breach clock and DPIA per regime; crisis resources per ISO country with an international directory as the fallback; a DPIA is the fifth counsel artifact [DECISION; which regimes counsel covers on day one is OPEN]

    Why A Saudi-only privacy design cannot serve a learner in Berlin

    Rejected PDPL-only (07 first draft); blocking signups from unreviewed countries

  21. ADR-21

    Payments

    Three USD plans as in ADR-04; hosted checkout; cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay worldwide; a merchant-of-record processor recommended (Paddle; Stripe with Stripe Tax as the fallback) because tax falls due in many countries; Mada and other regional methods later; founding price lock, not a founding plan; processor stays [OPEN for Muath] [DECISION on the model]

    Why No launch country means no single tax jurisdiction

    Rejected SAR-first, Mada-first processors (07 §4.14 first draft)

  22. ADR-22

    Open-item register

    The items only Muath can settle are listed once, in section 10 of this document, with the domain that waits on each; the other nine documents' section 10 lists point here where they overlap [DECISION]

    Why Thirteen plus eleven plus ten open lists in nine documents hid the few that block the build

    Rejected Each document keeping its own full list

  23. ADR-23

    Second-pass naming (integration, 2026-11-06 evening)

    After the nine revisions: Inngest event learner.extra_requested {learner_id, local_date, slot, episode_id}; episodes.slot (1 = the drop, 2 and 3 = daily_extra); terminal state superseded for unused extras and swapped dailies; turn_index everywhere; cost/reconcile-spend hourly; rater.pinned_until = 2027-02-16 (05's cohort-aligned first window); object keys learners/{lid}/... with the bucket-level ephemeral/ prefix (04 D15); client_perf.first_sentence_ms; A3 facts stored usable; no zeroization claim anywhere

    Why Nine agents revising in parallel chose different spellings for the same new things; one spelling per thing before the technical design reads them

    Rejected day_seq, learner.next_requested, unused, turn_n, cost/reconcile, a 2027-01-19 rater window, episodes/{learner_id}/ prefixes

Open for Muath

Section 10: the register of everything only you can settle, across all ten documents.

  1. Home region and the legal view on storing learner voice (counsel, by 2026-11-17)

    ADR-08 makes eu-central-1 the default for a worldwide cohort and keeps me-central-1 as a one-day config move. Counsel confirms: where a worldwide cohort's voice recordings and transcripts may rest, the transfer basis for the US model, voice and recognition vendors (standard contractual clauses plus consent is the working assumption), whether voice used only for the learner's own progress counts as biometric data under GDPR or PDPL, and whether the in-session verbatim "yes" is explicit consent for sensitive-class facts. [01, 07]

  2. Which jurisdictions the beta covers on day one (counsel, by 2026-11-17)

    Recommended: EU and UK GDPR, Saudi PDPL and the generic baseline; signups from unreviewed countries accepted under the generic notice. Also the DPIA, the consent and notice copy per native language, and the reviewer and tagger confidentiality agreement. [07, 06]

  3. Payment processor and legal entity (by 2026-12-02)

    A merchant-of-record processor (Paddle, about 5%) handles tax in every country; Stripe with Stripe Tax (about 3% plus registrations) needs an entity in a Stripe country. Trial without a card (PRD 18 #4) is assumed. [07, 01, 06]

  4. Tier prices against the break-even

    At the post-doubling floor stack ($0.43 to $0.45 a session) the 2 and 3 a day plans lose money on a learner who uses every episode (break-even $0.42). Options: keep the prices and accept the loss on full-use learners (typical use is about half), raise 2 a day to $34.99, or commit to the voice contract in #5. The exit document on 2026-12-22 carries the measured number. [08]

  5. The last lever to $0.35 and the voice contract (by 2026-12-22)

    A negotiated voice rate near $12 to $15 per million characters (no product change), read-aloud and recap rendered on first tap (about four engineer-days across 02, 03 and 06 and a 2 to 4 s wait), or 5-minute calls (in tension with the 4-minute speech gate). If Gemini wins the English blind test, decide the January plan now: swap the episode voice to the runner-up on 2027-01-01 with the on-air line, or ask Google for a rate. [08, 02, 03, 06]

  6. Haiku 5.5 as the call host

    if the 50-call comparison in build week 4 shows no difference in move-contract violations, talk share and the human read; worth $0.046 a session. [03, 08]

  7. On-call for a worldwide cohort

    With learners in every timezone there is no quiet hour. Accept that in the beta a one-engineer rota answers overnight P1s with the product's fallbacks (yesterday's episode stays; voice notes) and a human at the next working hour, or fund a second responder; and approve Better Stack's on-call plan (about $30 a month). [09, 01]

  8. Web or native for the beta

    The architecture builds the installable web app plus the private feed (decisions.md §7's recommendation); the switch-to-native triggers (calls abandoned on the move, feed-to-call handoff lost) are measured from day one. This stays open as the coordinator asked; nothing in this pass changes it. [06, 01]

  9. Fixed infrastructure budget: about $1,000 to $1,300 a month

    (01's $800 to $1,100 plus staging kept up through the beta, ADR-16), plus vendor minimums (voice winner's paid tier $100 to $330, LiveKit Ship $50, Langfuse Core up to $59, Speechace, Better Stack $30) and the Anthropic prepaid balance (about $2,500 for the beta's first month at the upper plans' usage). [01, 08, 09]

  10. Second engineer, and the week-6 slips

    (01 §10 #5). Decide by 2026-11-18. [01]

  11. The reviewers

    A paid native reader per target language (English first; about 4 hours a day at 100 learners, less with 09's cut), who also performs the language gate's writing check; the claims auditor (Muath or a second trained person); the native-language law-scan reader (Muath for Arabic). [09, 02]

  12. Crisis protocol details (counsel, by 2026-12-15)

    Clip wording per pair, the per-country resources list and the international fallback, whether the keyword list may fire alone (ADR default: the host confirms first, 2-second cap), whether SB 243 applies, and whether Muath wants to be told of activations and write personally. [07, 03]

  13. Consents for Milestone 0 data

    (01 §10 #11): the vendor-test addendum before any clip leaves (by 2026-11-01), and whether the pilot consent covers re-onboarding the 15 learners in build week 5 and keeping de-identified fixtures. [01, 07, 09]

  14. Processors to approve

    (07 §4.9 as revised): Langfuse Cloud EU under ADR-09, Resend with tracking off, Sentry EU, Vercel, LiveKit Cloud, Inngest EU, the verbatim recognizer with minimum retention; and the Anthropic zero-data-retention request. [07, 01, 08]

  15. Smaller items that stay with their documents

    archive growth (01 §10 #7), WhatsApp invites (01 #8), the 30 s median / 45 s p95 budgets (01 #9), the PRD 3.10 amendment for two transactional emails (01 #10), the brand domain (01 #13), research rows after deletion (04 and 07: deleted), the export passphrase (04), the "very disappointed" question mechanism (09), pulling a shipped episode (09 R4), the stop line's owner and window (08, 09: a human, 48 hours), the daily cap formula (08), cost figures in the review packet (08), staff passkeys and a penetration test before public launch (07), age verification beyond self-attestation (07), waitlist retention (07), the first rater window to 2027-02-16 (05 §10 #6), and the fact that renewal_m2 for the first cohort is not readable before the 2027-01-26 gate review (09): the review reads it as "not yet".
