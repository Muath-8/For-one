# Client app, podcast feed and delivery

*Architecture domain 6 of 9 for the For one beta. Written 2026-10-16, revised 2026-10-17 after design review, and revised again 2026-11-06 against the 2026-10-23 decisions (decisions.md), the second review pass (the conformance review in `_review-findings.md`; no skeptic review of this document exists) and 10-decision-records.md. Sources, in the order ADR-01 gives them: decisions.md, concept-v2 sections 5 to 10, PRD-v2 (Implementation PRD v3.0) where the first two are silent, the UI/UX and voice lenses, and the Milestone 0 kit. "PRD x.y" means PRD-v2 part x.y. Tags: [DECISION] decided here; [ASSUMPTION] assumed because the sources are silent; [OPEN for Muath] only the owner can settle. It covers everything the learner touches that is not the live call's audio pipeline: the installable web app, the player, the word sheet, the private podcast feed, web push and email, auth, bidirectional text, the client-to-backend API, realtime for the drop, state management, analytics, accessibility and the sample-episode landing page. It builds on the five sibling documents already written (01 topology, 02 pipeline, 03 live call, 04 learner model, 05 assessment) and reuses their names; where it changes one of their decisions it says so in section 9.*

*What changed in this revision, in one paragraph. The 20 findings of the conformance review are applied (section 11 lists each): the cache warm moves into the service worker's push handler and the cold path is the sized path; the eight promises are rendered at onboarding and in Controls; Salma gets a thread (`/chat/salma`) and typed asks (`salma_ask`) for the rhythm, the season goal and the gap note; "delete all recordings" has an endpoint; the pilot keeps its transcript open; `share_token`, `<googleplay:block>`, `idea_shown`, `DoorState.available`, the chat-opener trigger, `age_attested`, the "AI" mark, the same-origin cover, deferred `skipWaiting`, the `/show?k=` exemption and the `no-store` manifest are in. The rulings of 10-decision-records.md are applied: Salma's notes, the margin note, the polish card and the notes door are gone (ADR-06); the interface language and direction come from the learner's native-language package and nothing is Arabic-only (ADR-02); three USD plans, `plan.episodes_per_day`, the "next one" control and `POST /v1/episodes/next` (ADR-04, 05); show audio is served by presigned URLs (ADR-12); `residence_country` is asked at A9 and the crisis card is per country (ADR-20); the checkout fits Paddle or Stripe (ADR-21); the Vercel region follows `HOME_REGION` (ADR-08); the release window is the learner-drop rule (ADR-16); every date moved 16 days (ADR-07). Web versus native for the beta stays open (10-decision-records §10 item 8); the PWA is the default and section 8 says what changes if Muath picks native.*

## 1. Summary and the decisions in one table

The client is a thin, logic-free installable web app whose job is to play audio well, show text in two directions well, and send honest events. Every rule about the learner (doors, the allowance by plan, consent, counts) lives in `api`; the client reflects it. The interface speaks the learner's native language (strings, direction and fonts from `packages/native/<lang>/`, ADR-02); the content is in the target language. The screen shows the producer (Salma), the series and the weekly proof, never a correction list: there are no notes, no margin notes and no polish card (ADR-06), and "explain when asked" is a chat affordance to Salma. The private podcast feed is a first-class second channel that carries the part of the product phone browsers do worst (lock-screen listening on the commute) and hands the learner back to the app for the call through one link. Three message kinds leave the product and the service worker refuses to render anything else.

### 3.4 Data flows

**A. A normal morning (dropped episode).** At the learner's `drop_at` (their own `tz`; learners are in every timezone, ADR-16) `day/next` calls `feed.publish` (sets `feed_items.published_at`, bumps the feed ETag) then `push.drop_notification` (one payload `{kind: drop, episode_id, manifest_version, title, url}` to every live subscription of the learner). The episode was normally produced overnight in batch while the app was closed, so no page was open to warm the cache; the SW's `push` handler therefore runs `event.waitUntil(cache.addAll(warm_assets))` for this `(episode_id, manifest_version)` before `showNotification` (Chrome and iOS both run the handler with the app closed; under `saveData` only the JSON assets are warmed). The phone shows "Karim vs Mona: the interview (6 min)". Tap → `notificationclick` → `clients.openWindow('/today?entry=push')` → the PWA loads the shell from precache, reads the cached `TodayDoc` (stale-while-revalidate) and shows the hero in the `ready` state: the cover and ▶ Play (PRD 3.4; law 11: the tap never starts audio). On Play, the page finds `episode.m4a` in `forone-media-v1`, builds a Blob URL and starts from local bytes; `play_start_ms` is measured from the Play tap to the first audible sample and logged with `{context: daily, source: blob|network}` so the warm and cold paths are measured separately. When the warm did not run (no push permission, `saveData`, the SW evicted, the first open after a reinstall) the same tap streams from the presigned URL (4.4.4 case 2): this cold path is the one the 2 s budget is sized on (section 6). `play` events batch every 2 s to `POST /v1/events`; the half-episode mark makes the day active server-side. When the episode ends and the throw is answered or dismissed, Today shows "Next one" if the plan's allowance has room (D19).

**B. The same morning on the feed.** The podcast app polled `/f/{token}.xml` at some point after 07:40, saw the new `<item>` (its `<guid>` is the episode id), downloaded `/f/{token}/{id}.mp3`. `api` logged a `feed_fetches` row and nothing else. The learner listens on the bus with the phone locked. The episode ends with Salma's 5 s tag; the notes show two links. Tapping the call link opens Safari at `/call/{episodeId}?k=...`; `web` posts `k` to `POST /v1/links/exchange`, receives a limited session cookie, strips `k` with `history.replaceState`, logs `app_open {entry: "feed_link"}` with `feed_to_call_ms` started at link open, shows the planning card if Read is not done, and starts the call (live-call domain). The article link `/show/{episodeId}?k=...&door=read` takes the same exchange. After the call the chat works in this Safari context too; Controls shows "sign in to change settings". An expired or revoked `k` shows the sign-in screen and then routes to the requested episode, never a dead end.

**C. First open.** Invite link `/i/{code}` → `GET /v1/invites/{code}` returns the waitlist answers (including `native_language` and `target_language`) → the shell loads `GET /v1/package/ui?lang=<native>` and sets `<html lang dir>` → A1 (static, the 18+ checkbox) → A2 Talk → `POST /v1/onboarding/start {invite_code, tz, age_attested: true, text_shown_version, native_language, target_language, mode}` creates the `pre_account` learner, sets the `forone_s` cookie with `scope: pre_account`, returns `{learner_id, call: {room, token}}` → the Salma call (live-call domain, in the native language) → A4 to A7 → A8 (Salma's one closing sentence, then the eight promises as one scroll) → A9 `POST /v1/auth/{otp|apple|google}` claims the learner (`learners.status = trial`, `trial_started_at`, `subscriptions.plan = two_a_day` for the trial), `PUT /v1/me {birth_date, residence_country, drop_time_local, anchor_text}` (under 18 → 403 with the plain message and the pre-account row deleted; `residence_country` is asked, never inferred, ADR-20) → A10 ideas, then the install card: the page sets `<link rel="manifest" href="/manifest.webmanifest">` whose route mints a `handoff_tokens` row and returns `start_url: "/today?h={token}"`; the "copy feed link" button calls `GET /v1/feed/url`.

**D. Offline.** The SW serves the shell; today's episode plays from Cache Storage through a Blob URL; `TodayDoc` comes from the HTTP cache (stale) with an offline banner; doors that need the network (call, chat, swap) show one plain line; events queue in IndexedDB (`forone-queue`) with their original `occurred_at`; on reconnect the page (or Background Sync on Chrome) replays them with `X-Forone-Replay: 1`; the server honours the original timestamps (data domain 4.1.4).

Build: `next build`; CI runs `tsc`, ESLint (including the law rule), Playwright on iOS Safari and Android Chrome via BrowserStack for the player and RTL snapshots (week 3 onward), and `openapi-typescript` against the staging `api`'s `/v1/openapi.json` (a drift fails the build). The installed PWA may run yesterday's build for a day, so `api` keeps one release of backward compatibility (topology 4.8). The SW does **not** `skipWaiting` on install: the page messages the waiting SW to activate only when the player is idle and no call or recording is active, then reloads; the previous precache is kept until that activation so an open page can still lazy-load its route chunks after a deploy (4.6 rule 4). `middleware.ts` routes by host: `forone.app` → `(public)`; `app.forone.app` → `(app)`; an `(app)` route without a `forone_s` cookie redirects to `/onboarding/a1` except `/call/[id]?k=`, `/show/[id]?k=` and `/today?h=`, which exchange their token first and then remove it from the URL and history with `history.replaceState`. `/today` with a full `forone_s` cookie already present strips `h` without calling the exchange (every later launch of the installed app carries the consumed `h` in its `start_url`); only a launch with no cookie calls `handoff/exchange`.

### 4.3 The API contract

Base URL `https://api.forone.app/v1`. Cookie `forone_s` (HttpOnly, Secure, `SameSite=Lax`, `Domain=.forone.app`, `Path=/`, `Max-Age` 90 days rolling, value = opaque 32-byte id of an `auth_sessions` row). ### 4.4 The player

**4.4.1 Inputs.** `EpisodeDoc` (title, cast, kind, state, `player_defaults: {transcript_open: boolean, speed: 0.75|0.85|1.0, speech_rate}`, `media: {app, scenes[], article, recap, timings, glosses, package}` as asset names, `targets` and `new_words` lemma lists for Huh? glossing, `ask_followed`), the pipeline's `AudioManifest` (`timings.json`: `lines[]` with `sentences[]` and `words[]`, `segments[]` with `seam` and `stream_url`, `pause_no_capture`, `timing_quality: "word"|"sentence"`, `article_words[]`, `shadow_lines[]`) and `WordIndex` (`glosses.json`). On load the client builds two flat, sorted arrays: `words: {s, e, line_id, i}[]` and `sentences: {s, e, sent_id, line_id, text}[]`, and a `Map<line_id, Map<i, gloss_ref>>` from the index. For a 900-word script this is under 5 ms.

**4.4.2 State machine** (`PlayerStore`, Zustand, one instance app-wide so Today, Show and the lock screen share it):

```
states: idle → loading → ready → playing ⇄ paused → ended → thrown
                                   playing --(huh)--> huh_replay --(sentence end)--> playing
                                   any --(switch episode)--> loading
context: { episode_id, source: "blob"|"network"|"scenes", scene_index, position_ms, speed, transcript_open,
           huh: {sent_id, restore_speed} | null, mark_pending: boolean, media_session_owner: "player"|"call" }
```

Transitions that matter for the laws: `ended` fires the `completion` event (`play` with `position_ms = duration`) and moves to `thrown` when the manifest's last line is the throw; in `thrown` the three choices (Call now, Read the article first, Later) are shown and the Media Session `play` action is a no-op (PRD 4 rule 2: pressing play on the headphones after the throw does nothing). There is no `next` state and no queue; the "Next one" request of ADR-05 lives on Today (D19), appears only once the player has left `playing` for `thrown` or `ended` and the throw's choice is made, and is never reachable from the player, the lock screen or the feed. "Later" simply closes the sheet; the Today doors show Call as `not_started`.

**4.4.3 Audio elements.** Three `HTMLAudioElement`s created once per app load and unlocked on the first user gesture (play then pause on a 100 ms silent data URI; iOS allows programmatic `play()` later only on elements that have been started by a gesture): `main` (the episode or article or recap), `clip` (word sheet, marked moments, proof evidence, shadow lines) and `aux` (the second element of the scene handoff while streaming). `preservesPitch = true`. `playsinline`. `crossOrigin = "anonymous"` when the source is a presigned URL (ADR-12); `use-credentials` only for the learner-voice route on `api`.

**4.4.4 Source selection.** (1) If `forone-media-v1` holds the full `episode.m4a` for this manifest version: `main.src = URL.createObjectURL(await (await cache.match(url)).blob())` (`source: "blob"`; the Blob URL is kept for the episode's lifetime and revoked on `switch episode`; `clip` shares it). (2) Else if `manifest.timing_quality == "word"` and the app file exists on the server: `main.src` = the presigned URL the media route redirects to (`source: "network"`; ADR-12: show audio is `sse_kms_env` and served from S3 or the CDN, with Range for seeking), and the page starts the warm (4.6 rule 1) of the full file in the background so the next open is case 1. This is the common path on a morning when the SW warm did not run and the one the 2 s budget is sized on (section 6). (3) Else (streaming): `main.src = scene-{s0}`, `aux.src = scene-{s1}` preloaded (presigned scene URLs from `episode.manifest`); on `main.ended` swap roles at the seam (the pipeline guarantees a sting or beat at every segment end); when `episode.manifest` arrives on the stream with the full file, the player switches to it at the next seam, seeking to the same absolute position (`segment.start_ms + position`). While streaming, only the highlight granularity degrades: `sentences[]` come from the manifest's sentence-level estimate, `data-active` moves per sentence, and word taps still resolve through the index. The fold is not changed by streaming: it stays governed by `player_defaults.transcript_open`, with an explicit override `transcript_open: true` for `kind: pilot` (`EpisodeCard.transcript_open_override`, set by the pipeline's `package.json`) so A6 shows the transcript with its hint on first contact (PRD 2.2 A6). Under `timing_quality: sentence`, 🔊 on a word plays the word's sentence, not the word (4.5).

**4.4.5 Sync loop.** `requestAnimationFrame` while playing (and a 250 ms `setInterval` when the tab is hidden, which iOS throttles but does not stop for playing audio): `t = main.currentTime * 1000 + scene_offset`; binary search `words` for the active word; if it changed, move the `data-active` attribute (one DOM write) and, when the active line changes, scroll the transcript so the current line sits at 40% of the viewport (`scrollIntoView({block: "center"})` only when the learner has not scrolled in the last 3 s). Word spans are rendered once; nothing re-renders per frame.

### 4.5 The word sheet

Tap on a word span → `index.script.get(line_id).get(i)` → `gloss_ref` → `index.glosses[gloss_ref]` → render `{meaning_native, pos, register_note_native, example_target}` plus the sentence the word came from (`sentences[]` by `sent_id`) → measure `word_sheet_open_ms` from `pointerup` to first paint (`PerformanceObserver` on the sheet's `element-timing`), logged in `client_perf` when it exceeds 300 ms. 🔊 → `clip.src = main.src` (the same Blob URL or presigned URL), `clip.currentTime = (w.s - 60) / 1000`, play, pause at `w.e + 60` ms (a `timeupdate` guard plus a `setTimeout` at the expected duration). When the manifest has only sentence-level timing (`timing_quality: sentence`, every streaming kind until the republish), the word has no `[s, e]` of its own: 🔊 plays the sentence that contains it (`sentence.s` to `sentence.e`) and the sheet says so in one word ("sentence"); the gloss and Keep are unchanged. Keep → `keep {unit_id, sense_key, sentence_text}` and an optimistic tick. The sheet is a bottom sheet that does not pause the main audio unless 🔊 is tapped (then `main` pauses for the clip and resumes).

Phrase selection: `pointerdown` on a word, `pointermove` across spans (with `touch-action: pan-y` so vertical scroll still works), `pointerup` → the span `[i, j]` within one line; if any index entry in the span carries a `chunk_id` whose extent equals `[i, j]`, show the chunk's gloss (`index.glosses[chunk gloss_ref]`); otherwise show the per-word glosses stacked in order and log one `tap` per word with `chunk: false`. A 350 ms long-press on a single word selects it for the drag (iOS otherwise opens the text-selection UI; the transcript has `user-select: none`).

Misses: when `index.missing[]` contains the lemma, the sheet shows the sentence at once and calls `GET /v1/gloss?lemma=&sense_key=` (the pipeline's `gloss-service.lookup`) with a 1.5 s timeout and a plain "no meaning yet" fallback; this is the only network path on tap and it is rare.

### 4.6 Service worker and caching

Serwist configuration (`sw.ts`):

```ts
const sw = new Serwist({
  precacheEntries: self.__SW_MANIFEST,              // the Next.js shell: html, js, css, fonts, icons; the native package's static Salma clips used by onboarding UI
  skipWaiting: false, clientsClaim: true, navigationPreload: true,   // activation is deferred until the player is idle and no call or recording runs (rule 4)
  runtimeCaching: [
    { matcher: ({url}) => url.hostname === "api.forone.app" && /\/v1\/episodes\/[^/]+\/media\//.test(url.pathname),
      handler: new CacheFirst({ cacheName: "forone-media-v1",
        plugins: [ new RangeRequestsPlugin(),                                     // 206 from a cached 200, for the rare network-source case that hits the cache
                   new ExpirationPlugin({ maxEntries: 40, purgeOnQuotaError: true }),
                   new CacheableResponsePlugin({ statuses: [200] }) ] }) },      // never cache a 206 itself; the 302 to the presigned URL is followed and the final 200 is stored under the /v1 key
    { matcher: ({url}) => url.pathname === "/v1/today" || url.pathname.startsWith("/v1/shelf") || url.pathname === "/v1/package/ui",
      handler: new StaleWhileRevalidate({ cacheName: "forone-docs-v1" }) },
    { matcher: ({url}) => url.pathname.startsWith("/covers/"), handler: new CacheFirst({ cacheName: "forone-media-v1" }) },
    { matcher: ({request}) => request.destination === "font", handler: new CacheFirst({ cacheName: "forone-fonts-v1" }) },
  ],
  fallbacks: { entries: [{ url: "/offline", matcher: ({request}) => request.mode === "navigate" }] },
});
```

Rules the page enforces on top:

1. **Warm today's episode**, in three places, all through one `warm(episode_id, manifest_version)` function in `lib/media/` that is also bundled into the SW: (a) **the SW's `push` handler**, on a `kind: drop` payload carrying `episode_id` and `manifest_version`: `event.waitUntil(warm(...).then(showNotification))`; this is the warm that matters, because tomorrow's episode is normally produced overnight after the pick and becomes ready while the app is closed, and both Chrome and iOS run the push handler with the app closed; (b) the page, on `today.changed {reason: episode_ready|extra_ready}` while it is in the foreground and on `session_closed` the evening before (the pipeline publishes to the app the moment the episode is ready; the drop time only controls feed and push); (c) the page, after a Play that streamed (4.4.4 case 2), so the next open is case 1. `warm` fetches each asset's `/v1/episodes/{id}/media/{asset}` URL (following the 302 to the presigned URL, ADR-12), checks `response.ok` and a full `Content-Length`, and `cache.put()`s it under the stable `/v1` URL into `forone-media-v1`; the set is `web.sw.warm_assets` (`episode.m4a`, `timings.json`, `glosses.json`, `package.json`, `article.m4a`, `recap.m4a`, the cover); on a metered connection (`navigator.connection.saveData`, read in the SW too) only `web.sw.warm_assets_metered` (the JSON assets) are warmed and the audio streams. The warm is best effort: no push permission, `saveData`, an evicted SW or a reinstall all leave the cache cold, which is why the cold path is the sized one (section 6) and why `play_start_ms` carries `source`. `web.sw.cache_episodes_n = 3` keeps today's and the last two; older entries are evicted by the plugin and the archive streams them on demand. Playback reads the cached entries through `caches.match()` and Blob URLs (4.4.4), so playback never depends on the SW's fetch handler being invoked for a media element, which Safari has historically done inconsistently.
2. **Never cache a partial response.** A 206 from `api` is passed through and not stored (the plugin would otherwise store a truncated body).
3. **Quota.** `navigator.storage.estimate()` on app start; under 100 MB free, `cache_episodes_n` drops to 1; `persist()` is requested once after install (granted automatically to installed PWAs on Chrome; iOS ignores it but exempts home-screen apps from the 7-day eviction).
4. **Versioning and deferred activation.** Cache names carry a version; a new SW deletes caches with other versions on `activate`, and `activate` is deferred: the page listens for `updatefound`, and messages the waiting SW `{type: "activate"}` only when `PlayerStore` is `idle`, `paused` or `ended` and no call or recording is active, then reloads; until then the previous SW and its precache keep serving, so an open page can still lazy-load a route chunk after a deploy (the post-deploy chunk 404 never happens mid-playback or mid-call). Media keys are immutable per `(episode_id, manifest_version)` so a cached episode survives SW upgrades within a version; a republished manifest (streaming → word timings) changes the key and the page re-adds.
5. **Push and notification click** (4.8) live in the same SW, together with the warm of rule 1(a).
6. **Background Sync** (`forone-events`) on Chrome replays the event queue when the page is closed; Safari has no Background Sync, so the queue is replayed on the next open (the 24 h window matches the data domain's clock-skew rule for replays).

The event queue (`lib/events/queue.ts`): IndexedDB database `forone-queue`, store `batches` keyed by `batch_id`, each a JSON array of `EventIn`. `enqueue(event)` buffers in memory for 2 s or 50 events, writes the batch, and posts it; on success the batch is deleted; on network failure it stays; on `4xx` other than 429 the batch is marked `rejected` and reported to Sentry without payload text. Replays send `X-Forone-Replay: 1`. Batches older than `events.queue_ttl_h = 24` are dropped and counted. `occurred_at` is the device clock at the moment of the event; the server corrects skew.

`EpisodeMemory` (IndexedDB `forone-memory`, store `episodes` keyed by `episode_id`): `{position_ms, speed, transcript_opened_before, article_depth, last_opened_at}`; written on `pause`, `seek` and every 5 s while playing; mirrored to the server through `POST /v1/sessions/{id}/doors/{door}` on `pause` and `visibilitychange` so the position survives a reinstall (PRD 4 rule 4).

### 4.7 The private podcast feed

**Tokens.** `feed_tokens` is the topology's table (32 random bytes, base64url, one live row per learner). Reset inserts a new row and sets `revoked_at` on the old one; a revoked token returns `410 Gone` with a one-line XML comment in the learner's native language so the podcast app shows an error rather than silence. The feed URL is shown in A10 and Controls as `https://api.forone.app/f/{token}.xml` with a copy button and a deep link where the app supports one (Apple Podcasts accepts `podcast://api.forone.app/f/{token}.xml`; Pocket Casts accepts `pktc://subscribe/api.forone.app/f/{token}.xml`; Overcast accepts `overcast://x-callback-url/add?url=`; the instruction card tries the deep link first and shows the copy button always).

**Rendering.** `GET /f/{token}.xml` (no cookies, no CORS needed, `Cache-Control: private, max-age=300`, `ETag` = sha256 of the last `published_at` plus token row id):

`<itunes:block>Yes</itunes:block>`, `<googleplay:block>yes</googleplay:block>` and `<podcast:locked>yes</podcast:locked>` keep the feed out of directories (PRD 12.6; topology 3.3 and 3.6). Every string in the feed comes from the learner's native package (ADR-02); the channel title carries the learner's first name once. Item descriptions carry the two links and one fixed package sentence (`feed_only_note`: listening here does not start a new episode; open the app or tap a link for that), which is the topology's one-line note and the honest consequence of D6 (PRD 3.6.2). `api.feed` renders the links from `feed_links` rather than storing HTML, so a token reset re-mints every link; the pipeline's `notes_html` (podcast show notes, nothing to do with Salma's removed notes) is only a fallback. Items: the last 60 published episodes (the drop plus any `daily_extra` the learner has played, ADR-05: `episode/produce` calls `feed.publish` for an extra when its first `play` event arrives) plus the weekly proofs (`episodes.kind = proof`, published by `proof/make` through the same `feed.publish`), newest first; a `trial_ended` learner's feed keeps its items and gains no new ones. The feed never contains the sample or any text about the learner beyond the title.

**Enclosures.** `GET /f/{token}/{episodeId}.mp3`: validates the token, checks the episode belongs to the token's learner and `feed_items.published_at <= now()`, opens the S3 object the `media_objects {kind: feed_full, encryption: sse_kms_env}` row names (`forone-media/{lid}/episodes/{episodeId}/episode_feed.mp3`; show audio is SSE-KMS under the environment key, ADR-12) with the request's `Range` header, and streams it (this one show-audio route stays proxied rather than presigned because podcast apps cache enclosure URLs for days) with `Accept-Ranges`, `Content-Length`, `Content-Range`, `ETag` and `Cache-Control: private, max-age=31536000`. HEAD is supported (Apple Podcasts sends it). Every request appends a `feed_fetches` row.

```sql
create table feed_links (                -- D7: the links placed in feed item notes
  token        text primary key,         -- "fl_" + 24 random bytes base64url
  learner_id   uuid not null references learners(id) on delete cascade,
  feed_token   text not null references feed_tokens(token),   -- revoked with the feed token
  episode_id   uuid not null references episodes(id),
  created_at   timestamptz not null default now(),
  expires_at   timestamptz,              -- null: the link lives as long as its feed token (feed.link_ttl_days = null); it is revoked with the token anyway
  used_n       integer not null default 0
);
create table feed_fetches (              -- D6: operations and Part 18 #11, never learner events
  id bigserial primary key, learner_id uuid not null, feed_token text not null,
  kind text not null check (kind in ('xml','enclosure','cover')), episode_id uuid,
  ua_family text, range_start bigint, range_end bigint, bytes_sent bigint, status smallint,
  at timestamptz not null default now()
);
create index on feed_fetches (learner_id, at desc);
```

`links.mint(learner_id, episode_id)` is what the pipeline's publisher calls for the item notes (section 9); `api.feed` renders the item from `feed_items` plus the live `feed_links` row for that episode, so the stored `notes_html` is only a fallback. The link is minted with the feed token's lifetime rather than a 30-day TTL: a 60-item feed of a replayable archive would otherwise carry dead links, and the link is revoked with the token in any case.

**Counting (D6).** `feed_fetches` feeds three operational views: podcast apps in use by `ua_family` (Part 18 #11, reported weekly to Muath); enclosure fetch within 24 h of publish as a feed adoption measure; and the data domain's "feed episode credited once" rule, which it applies from `call_turn`, `chat_message` or `small_door` events and not from this table. No `play` event is ever written from a fetch; the data domain's `play.surface = "feed"` value is reserved and never emitted in the beta.

**Feed-to-call (D7).** `/call/{episodeId}?k=` and `/show/{episodeId}?k=` → `POST /v1/links/exchange`: the link's feed token must be live; `api` creates an `auth_sessions` row `{scope: "feed_link", episode_id, learner_id}` (14 days) and sets the cookie; `used_n` increments; the page removes `k` from the URL and history with `history.replaceState`. The page logs `app_open {entry: "feed_link", standalone: false, feed_to_call_ms: null}` and, when the call room joins, `client_perf {kind: "feed_to_call_ms", value}`. A limited session may: `GET /today`, `GET /episodes/{id}`, media, `POST /calls` for that session, the chat threads, `POST /events`. It may not: Controls, billing, export, delete, delete recordings, feed reset, `POST /episodes/next`, notebook edits (reads are fine). The screen shows a thin line "signed in through your feed link; sign in to change settings" only on the blocked surfaces. If the same browser already holds a full session, the link just opens the call. An expired or revoked `k` (the token was reset) shows the sign-in screen with the requested episode remembered in the URL, and routes there after sign-in.

**Return detection.** `api` sends `learner.return_detected` when a full or limited session's first request of the day finds no session event in 5 or more days; the feed XML and enclosure routes never send it (D6; a deviation from topology 3.6, section 9).

### 4.8 Web push, the stream, and the three message kinds

**Subscriptions.**

```sql
create table push_subscriptions (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  learner_id uuid not null references learners(id) on delete cascade,
  endpoint text not null unique, p256dh text not null, auth text not null,
  ua_hint text, standalone boolean not null default false,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(), last_ok_at timestamptz, failed_n smallint not null default 0, revoked_at timestamptz
);
```

The permission prompt is requested only from a tap on "turn on the drop notification" (A10's install card, or Controls), never on load; on iOS it is offered only when `navigator.standalone === true` (iOS 16.4+ allows web push only in the installed app) and the install card is shown first. `pushsubscriptionchange` re-subscribes and re-posts. A send that returns 404 or 410 sets `revoked_at`; three consecutive other failures do the same.

**Senders.** `api` has exactly three functions that can send to a learner, and nothing else in the codebase imports the push or email adapters:

| Kind | Function | Channel | Payload |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drop notification | `push.drop_notification(lid, episode_id)` from `day/next`; skipped when `consents.drop_notification = false` or no live subscription | Web push | `{kind: "drop", title: "Karim vs Mona: the interview (6 min)", url: "/today?entry=push", episode_id, manifest_version}` (the two ids let the SW warm the cache before showing the notification, 4.6 rule 1) |
| Gap note | `push.gap_note(lid)` and `email.send(kind: "gap_note")` from `gap/note`, once | Web push and email | `{kind: "gap_note", title: <the learner's own words, first 80 chars>, url: "/today?entry=gap_note"}`; the email body is the learner's text verbatim, signed by no host |
| Transactional | `email.send(kind ∈ {signin_code, receipt, trial_ending, payment_failed, export_ready, trial_deleting_soon, account_deleted, retention_warning, invite})` | Email (Resend, from `show@forone.app`) | templates in the native-language package (ADR-02); receipts and tax lines are the processor's (ADR-21); no host voice, no episode content |

The SW's `push` handler parses the payload, refuses any `kind` other than `drop` or `gap_note` (logs to Sentry, shows nothing), for `drop` first awaits the cache warm of 4.6 rule 1 (bounded by 25 s so the notification is never lost to the push service's handler timeout), and then calls `showNotification(title, {body: "", tag: kind + episode_id, renotify: false, data: {url}})`; `notificationclick` focuses an existing client or opens `url`. There is no `body`, no icon badge count, no action buttons. The drop push is sent with `TTL: 7200` so a phone off for a morning never receives a stale drop in the evening.

**The stream (D12).** `GET /v1/stream` holds one SSE connection per foreground learner; `api` `LISTEN`s on `forone_learner_{lid}` and relays `NOTIFY` payloads written by the modules that change the learner's visible state. Each message is `id: <seq>\nevent: <name>\ndata: <json>`; a comment line `: hb` every 20 s keeps the ALB's 60 s idle timeout from closing it. The client reconnects with `Last-Event-ID`; `api` replays from `learner_stream_log` (a 24 h ring table) or sends `event: resync` if the id is too old, after which the client refetches `TodayDoc`.

| event | data | client reaction |
|---|---|---|
| `today.changed` | `{reason: "episode_ready"|"extra_ready"|"session_closed"|"drop"|"swap"|"return"|"proof_ready"|"crisis"|"allowance"}` | refetch `/today`; warm the cache on `episode_ready`, `extra_ready` and `session_closed` (4.6 rule 1b) |
| `episode.manifest` | `{episode_id, manifest_version, timing_quality, full_ready, scene_urls[]}` | the streaming player switches source at the next seam (swap, return, pilot and `daily_extra` alike) |
| `chat.message` | `{host: "karim"|"mona"|"salma", message_index, kind: "host"|"crisis"|"salma_ask"|"ideas"}` | append to the thread if open, else a dot on the Chat door; `crisis` renders the package's resources card inline; the first `host` message after `doors/chat` closes the `chat_opener_ms` measure |
| `call.thread` | `{call_id, turn_index}` | voice-note reply arrived (live-call domain 5.1) |
| `ideas.ready` | `{plan_id}` | the three ideas appear at the chat closer or on Today |
| `controls.changed` | `{fields[]}` | refetch `/controls` and `/today` (a change made through Salma in her thread or by voice, D17; the matching `consent_change` is appended server-side) |

Nothing in the stream is required for correctness: every document is also polled with `If-None-Match` every 30 s while visible, and on `visibilitychange` to visible.

### 4.9 Auth

```sql
create table accounts (                       -- one row per external identity
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  learner_id uuid not null references learners(id) on delete cascade,
  provider text not null check (provider in ('email','apple','google')),
  subject text not null,                      -- normalized email, Apple sub, Google sub
  email text, verified_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  unique (provider, subject)
);
create table otp_codes (
  id uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  email citext not null, code_hash text not null,         -- argon2 of a 6-digit code
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(), expires_at timestamptz not null,   -- auth.otp_ttl_s = 600
  attempts smallint not null default 0, consumed_at timestamptz
);
create index on otp_codes (email, created_at desc);
create table auth_sessions (
  id text primary key,                        -- 32 random bytes base64url; the cookie value
  learner_id uuid not null references learners(id) on delete cascade,
  scope text not null check (scope in ('pre_account','full','feed_link')),
  episode_id uuid,                            -- feed_link only
  ua_hint text, standalone boolean,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(), last_seen_at timestamptz not null default now(),
  expires_at timestamptz not null, revoked_at timestamptz
);
create index on auth_sessions (learner_id) where revoked_at is null;
create table handoff_tokens (                 -- D10
  token text primary key, learner_id uuid not null references learners(id) on delete cascade,
  created_at timestamptz not null default now(), expires_at timestamptz not null, used_at timestamptz
);
```

Rules:

1. **Pre-account.** `POST /onboarding/start` creates `learners {status: pre_account}` and `auth_sessions {scope: pre_account, expires_at: now() + 24 h}`. `retention/daily` deletes `pre_account` learners older than 24 h with no claim (their pilot audio and intake with them). A pre-account session may do A1 to A10 and nothing on `/show`, `/notebook`, `/controls`.
2. **Claim at A9.** Any of the three sign-ins, when the current cookie is `pre_account`: if the identity is new, link `accounts` to this learner and set `status: trial`; if the identity already belongs to another learner (a returning learner who restarted onboarding), the response is `409 existing_show` and the client offers "open your existing show" (which signs in as that learner and abandons the pre-account one) or "keep this new show" (which is refused in the beta: one account, one show; the pre-account learner is deleted). `PUT /me {birth_date, residence_country}` follows; under 18 deletes the pre-account learner and shows the plain message (PRD 1.4); `residence_country` selects the regime row and the crisis resources (ADR-20) and is never inferred from the IP.
3. **Email code.** `POST /auth/otp/request` always returns 202; rate limits: 3 per email per 10 min, 10 per IP hash per hour; the email is kind `signin_code` with the 6-digit code and nothing else; `verify` allows 5 attempts per code; the code is consumed on success.
4. **Apple.** Services ID for `app.forone.app`, `response_mode=form_post` to `/v1/auth/apple/callback`, the state cookie carries the pre-account session id; Apple's private relay address is stored as `accounts.email`. Two practical requirements the week-3 checklist carries: the Apple Developer Program membership (section 10), and registering `forone.app` and `show@forone.app` under "Sign in with Apple for Email Communication" with SPF and DKIM on the sending domain, because mail to `@privaterelay.appleid.com` from an unregistered sender bounces, which would silently break sign-in codes, receipts and the export link for every Apple-relay learner.
5. **Google.** OIDC authorization code with PKCE, `prompt=select_account`; the Google Cloud OAuth consent screen must be verified for `forone.app` (section 10).
6. **Sessions.** Full sessions last `auth.session_days = 90`, rolling on use; `signout` revokes the row; account deletion revokes every row at once (data domain 4.12.2 step 1); `api` sets `forone.learner_id` for RLS from the row on every request.
7. **Install handoff (D10).** The manifest route is a Next.js route handler on `app.forone.app`: it reads `forone_s` (same-origin, so the browser sends it with the manifest fetch), forwards it to `POST /v1/handoff/mint` (one token per learner per 7 days), and returns the manifest JSON with `start_url: "/today?h=<token>"`, `scope: "/"`, `display: "standalone"`, `id: "/"`, icons, `theme_color` from the show colour, `lang` and `dir` from the UI bundle, with `Cache-Control: no-store, private` and `Vary: Cookie` so neither the browser nor Vercel's edge serves one learner's manifest to another. On first launch of the installed app, `/today?h=` finds no cookie in the PWA's jar, posts to `POST /v1/handoff/exchange`, receives a full-session cookie, logs `app_open {entry: "install_handoff", standalone: true}` and `pwa_install`, and strips `h` from the URL and history. Every later launch carries the same consumed `h` in its `start_url`; because a full `forone_s` cookie is now present, `/today` strips `h` without calling the exchange (4.1). A used or expired token with no cookie simply shows the sign-in screen (no email prefilled, no leak). Android Chrome shares the jar, so the token is unused there and expires.
8. **Feed link sessions** as in 4.7.

**4.10.1 A1 to A10.** The shell first loads `GET /v1/package/ui?lang=` for the invite's `native_language` (or `Accept-Language`, then `en`) and sets `<html lang dir>`; every onboarding string below is a package string. A2 requests the microphone only on Talk. A3 is the live-call domain's Salma call; the client renders fact cards from `ui.fact_card` messages and sends `fact_card.edit` and `fact_card.remove` on the data channel. A4 word check: 48 items from `OnboardingDoc.word_check` shown one at a time as big yes/no, a 75 s timer in code, `word_check_answer` per item, Skip as large as Start. A5 casting plays the cached hello clips from the package (no synthesis), swap cycles the host's pool, accent picks British or American with its sample; `PUT /onboarding/casting`; the same screen plays the hosts saying the learner's name from `OnboardingDoc.name.candidates[chosen].clip_url` with a "say it differently" button that cycles the three candidates through `PUT /onboarding/name` (the pipeline's name verification, its 4.8; shown always, emphasised when `verified == false`). When `consents.voice_storage == false`, the screen offers "keep this one recording" (`kept_explicitly: true`, PRD 12.5.2) before the first part. The same capture component serves "say it once", shadow, voice notes and the small door.

### 4.11 Bidirectional text

- `<html lang dir>` is set once per load from the cached `UiBundle` (`GET /v1/package/ui`; ADR-02; PRD 13.1 and 13.2): `lang` is the learner's `native_language`, `dir` is the package's direction, so the chrome is RTL only when the native language is RTL (`native/ar`) and LTR for `native/en` and the rest; the target language's direction (`UiBundle.target_dir`, LTR for English) governs the content surfaces. Nothing in `apps/web` hard-codes a language or a direction; the string bundle is the package's and the one pair that ships in the beta is built through the same mechanism. Tailwind with only logical utilities (`ps-`, `pe-`, `ms-`, `me-`, `start-`, `end-`, `text-start`); an ESLint rule forbids `pl-`, `pr-`, `ml-`, `mr-`, `left-`, `right-`, `text-left`, `text-right` in `apps/web`.
- Every block that may contain learner or host text renders through one `<Bidi as="p" text={...}/>` component that sets `dir="auto"` and `style="unicode-bidi: plaintext"`; an inline target-language run inside a native sentence (Salma quoting an English phrase in an Arabic answer) is wrapped in `<bdi>` by a tiny tokenizer that splits on script runs (the native script versus Latin) so punctuation stays with its run.
- Fixed-direction surfaces: the transcript, article, captions, host chat bubbles' target-language text, idea lines and the scrub bar take the target language's direction with `text-align: start`; the chrome around them follows the native direction. Timestamps `2:14 / 7:02` are LTR runs inside `<bdi>`.
- Digits: Western (0 to 9) by default, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` for times and counts; a native package may set `digits` otherwise [DECISION; section 10 item 5 for the brand call].
- Fonts: the native package names its UI font (`native/ar`: IBM Plex Sans Arabic; `native/en`: IBM Plex Sans) and IBM Plex Sans is the companion for Latin content, all through `next/font/local` with `font-display: swap`; RTL body `line-height: 1.7`, Latin transcript `1.5`; the transcript's current line is 20 px, the rest 17 px; minimum 16 px anywhere.
- Mirroring: directional icons (back, chevrons) use `rtl:-scale-x-100`; play, pause, ⟲ and the S button are not mirrored.
- Keyboard and input: chat input `dir="auto"`; the learner's bubbles use `plaintext` so an English sentence typed in an RTL UI aligns left and reads correctly.
- A Playwright snapshot suite renders the thirteen screens under `native/ar` (RTL chrome) and `native/en` (LTR chrome) with mixed-direction fixtures (an RTL Salma answer quoting English, an English bubble with an Arabic word, a word sheet) on both platforms and fails on diff.

### 4.13 The sample-episode landing page

`forone.app/` (SSR, cached at the edge for 10 min per language, served in the visitor's language by `Accept-Language` with English as the default and a language switch in the header, strings from `GET /v1/package/ui?lang=`; ADR-02): the promise sentence, "Hear your own show", the target-language choice (English is the only open target in the beta, ADR-03; the control exists so the mechanism is honest) and the three questions (name; something you could talk about for an hour; what you do), each with a text field and a hold-to-talk button (`MediaRecorder`, 20 s cap). Submit → `POST /v1/sample` multipart `{name, topic, job, topic_audio?, job_audio?, contact?, native_language, target_language}` with the first-party device cookie `forone_d` (random id set on first visit; `sample_requests.device_hash = sha256(it)`). `api` applies the two limits (one per device and per contact per 24 h) and returns `202 {sample_id}` or `429 {retry_at}` with Salma's plain sentence; voice answers are referenced as `answers_audio_ref` on `sample.requested` and transcribed inside the pipeline's sample path (section 9). The page polls `GET /v1/sample/{id}` every second: `{state: "making"|"streaming"|"ready"|"failed", segments: [{url}], duration_s}`; on `streaming` the minimal player (play, pause, scrub, no transcript, no speed) starts on scene 1 through the same two-element handoff as 4.4.4 case 3; the sample audio is public-bucket (`forone-media/samples/{sample_id}/`, service key, 30-day expiry) so no cookie is needed. `client_perf {kind: "sample_first_sound_ms"}` is posted anonymously to `POST /v1/telemetry` (no learner).

After the audio (Salma's ending in the visitor's native language is in the file): the waitlist form (`POST /v1/waitlist {name, contact: whatsapp|email, why, self_rating, country, native_language, target_language, sample_id}`) and "Keep it" which shows the share page URL `forone.app/s/{shareToken}` (`share_token` from `GET /v1/sample/{id}`, never the sample id, which is the poll handle; topology 9.5) and `navigator.share({url})` where available. The share page is public, `noindex`, with the Open Graph title from the package string `show_of(name)`, the OG image rendered from the typographic cover, and the same minimal player on `GET /s/{shareToken}.mp3`; it expires with the audio. Invites (`/i/{code}`) are minted by Muath from the waitlist in `/admin` and carry the answers into `POST /onboarding/start`; the channel that carries the invite link (email or WhatsApp) is the topology's open item 9; a used or expired code shows a plain line.

### 4.16 Payments touchpoints the client owns

**Plans (ADR-04).** `GET /billing` returns the three plans from `plans.yaml` (`one_a_day` $19.99, `two_a_day` $29.99, `three_a_day` $44.99, USD, monthly; `episodes_per_day` 1, 2, 3; tax added by the processor where due). The plan sheet (`/controls/plans`, also the body of the day-7 card) follows decisions.md §2's pricing psychology exactly and nothing beyond it: the three plans side by side with the middle plan marked "Recommended" and preselected (it is also the trial's plan, so the learner continues what they had), the top plan as the anchor at the same price per episode as the middle one, the line "about $1 a day" under the middle plan, the tutor comparison as one sentence from the package, the founding line ("the first 100 members keep this price while subscribed", shown while `founding_seq <= 100` is still available; `founding_locked` is a lock, not a plan), and "cancel in one tap" as a visible promise that `POST /v1/billing/portal` keeps. Never: a countdown, scarcity, guilt, a pre-ticked upgrade, a dark cancel path. Prices are rendered from the document, never hard-coded; the law lint rule bans a hard-coded `$` string in `apps/web`.

**Checkout (ADR-21).** The processor is [OPEN for Muath] (10-decision-records §10 item 3; decision by 2026-12-02, the start of build week 3): Paddle as merchant of record is recommended because tax falls due in many countries with no launch country; Stripe with Stripe Tax is the fallback. The client is designed so either fits without a change: it sees only a hosted checkout. `POST /v1/billing/checkout {plan}` returns the processor's URL (Paddle's hosted checkout or Stripe Checkout); the return URL is `/controls?billing=done`, where the client refetches `/billing` and `TodayDoc` (the processor's webhook sets `subscriptions.plan`, `learners.status` and `founding_locked`); `POST /v1/billing/portal` opens the processor's management page for plan changes and cancellation. The day-7 card on Today and the day-6 `trial_ending` email are driven by `trial_started_at`; the trial runs on `two_a_day` without a card (PRD 18 #4 assumption, 10-decision-records §10 item 3). Apple Pay and Google Pay on the hosted page are the processor's: with Stripe, Apple Pay needs the domain-association file served at `https://app.forone.app/.well-known/apple-developer-merchantid-domain-association` (a static file in `public/`), registered with Stripe when it is chosen; with Paddle the overlay or hosted checkout runs on Paddle's domain and no file is needed, so the file is kept in `public/` either way and is harmless. Mada and other regional methods come later (ADR-21); the client never renders a card form.

## 5. Failure modes, fallbacks and degraded modes

| Failure | Detected by | Behaviour | Learner sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offline on the bus | `navigator.onLine`, fetch errors | Shell and today's episode from cache (Blob URL); events queue; Call, Chat, Swap disabled with one line; Today from the doc cache marked stale | The episode plays; "no signal, the call waits" on the call door |
| Episode not warmed (no push permission, `saveData`, SW evicted, reinstall; the common case when the overnight warm did not run) | cache miss on Play | Streams from the presigned URL (`source: network`, 4.4.4 case 2); first bytes arrive in 200 to 600 ms from S3 or the CDN, play starts on `canplay`; the warm runs in the background | Up to 1 s longer start; the sized path: p95 under 2 s on 4G, measured in the week-3 device matrix |
| Push-handler warm did not finish before the notification (slow network, 25 s cap) | `web.sw.push_warm_timeout_s` | The notification is shown anyway; the warm continues in the handler's remaining budget or the page completes it on open | Nothing, or the cold path above |
| Allowance used (`POST /episodes/next` → `409 allowance_used`) or the extra's on-demand path is paused by the spend mode (ADR-14) | the 409 code | Today hides "Next one" and shows one plain sentence; listening, the article, the chat and round two keep working (unlimited on every plan) | "That was today's last new one" or "the next one is later today" |
| Cache Storage read fails or throws (private mode, cleared site data) | `caches.match()` rejects or returns nothing | Fall to the network source for this play; re-warm later | Nothing |
| iOS evicts Cache Storage (site unused 7 days, browser tab not installed) | cache miss on open | Re-warm; the archive streams; nothing is lost because the server holds everything | Slower first play that day |
| Quota exceeded | `QuotaExceededError`, `purgeOnQuotaError` | Keep only today's episode; drop fonts cache last | Nothing |
| `api` down | fetch 5xx or timeout | Cached `TodayDoc` read-only; events queue 24 h; calls cannot start (voice notes also need `api`) | "The studio is unreachable; your episode still plays" |
| Vercel down | shell fails to load | Installed PWA runs from precache (shell cached); a fresh browser gets nothing; the feed and media are on `api` and unaffected | Installed learners notice nothing; the feed works |
| SSE drops (ALB, sleep, roaming) | `EventSource.onerror` | Reconnect with backoff 1 s to 30 s and `Last-Event-ID`; the 30 s ETag poll continues regardless | Nothing |
| Push subscription expired or permission revoked | 404/410 from the push service; `Notification.permission` | Row revoked; Controls shows "notifications are off on this phone" with the button; the feed's own alert remains | One line in Controls |
| Drop push arrives but the episode was swapped or replaced by a return episode before open | `episode_id` in the payload differs from `TodayDoc` | The page ignores the payload id and shows Today | The current episode |
| Feed token leaked or forwarded | learner reports, or `feed_fetches` from many `ua_family` values | Controls "reset the link" revokes the token and every `feed_links` row; a 410 with a native-language comment to the old subscribers | New link to paste |
| Podcast app refuses private feeds or the enclosure format (Part 18 #11) | `feed_fetches` never arrive for that `ua_family`, or the week-3 test fails | A10 and Controls name only apps that work; the app player and push remain; `feed.enclosure_format` is the switch if a named app needs the other container | The instruction card |
| Feed link opened in a browser with an old full session for another learner (shared phone) | `links.exchange` sees a mismatch | The exchange replaces the session with the limited one for the link's learner and says so | "Opened {first name}'s call link" |
| Feed link expired or revoked (token reset) | `links.exchange` → 410 | Sign-in screen with the requested episode kept in the URL; routed there after sign-in | One sign-in, then the episode |
| Cookie jar split on iOS (Safari vs installed) | sign-in screen in the PWA | D10 handoff; if the token was consumed or expired, sign in once with the email code; Android unaffected | At most one email code |
| Sign-in code to an Apple relay address bounces | Resend bounce webhook | Alert; the learner is shown "use Google or another email" on the next attempt; prevented by the domain registration in 4.9 rule 4 | One plain line |
| Mic permission denied | `NotAllowedError` | Onboarding falls to Type mode; calls fall to voice notes only if the mic later works, else the chat door; the recording screen says what to change in system settings | Plain line with the system settings path |
| Screen Wake Lock unsupported | `navigator.wakeLock` missing | Live-call domain's silent video loop | A one-line notice |
| Background play stops on iOS | `pause` without learner action | Logged `client_perf bg_play_stop`; the lock-screen control resumes it; the feed is the fallback channel | A paused episode |
| Scene handoff gap audible on a streaming episode | manifest `seam == null` for a segment (should not happen) | 150 ms crossfade on `aux` start | A tiny gap at a scene change |
| Recording upload fails mid-task | fetch error on `POST /recordings` | The blob stays in `forone-queue` and is retried for 24 h; the task continues; the next part is not blocked | Nothing, or "saved, will send when there is signal" |
| Stale shell after a deploy | SW `updatefound` | The waiting SW is activated by the page only when the player is idle and no call or recording is active, then the page reloads; the old precache serves route chunks until then (4.6 rule 4); `api` compatible for one release; deploys happen only inside the learner-drop release window (ADR-16) | Nothing |
| Presigned URL expires mid-play (10 minutes, a paused episode resumed later) | media element error 403 | The player re-requests `/v1/episodes/{id}/media/{asset}`, gets a fresh URL, seeks to the saved position; a cached episode never meets this | Nothing, or a 300 ms gap on resume |
| Processor webhook late after checkout | `/controls?billing=done` refetch still shows `trial` | The client polls `/billing` for 60 s and shows "confirming"; the webhook is the truth and Today never blocks | One line, then the plan |
| Clock badly wrong on the device | server skew check | `occurred_at` corrected server-side; `client_perf` values unaffected (durations) | Nothing |
| Learner deletes the account from another device | `410` on any request | The PWA clears caches, IndexedDB and the cookie and shows the deletion sentence | One screen |
| Trial ended without paying | `TodayDoc.learner.status == trial_ended` | Today shows Salma's sentence and the plan sheet (D18); Show, Notebook, Controls (export, delete) keep working for 90 days; the feed publishes nothing new | A quiet Today |
| Sample abuse (many devices) | `sample_requests` rate by IP hash over 1 h | `api` returns 429 with `retry_at`; the pipeline's $0.05 ceiling bounds the damage per sample | Salma's one plain sentence |
