# Reviewer and tagger confidentiality agreement

> **Draft for counsel. Not legal advice.** The one-page agreement 07 §4.13 requires before a reviewer or tagger's first login (`staff_accounts.agreement_signed_at`), drafted 2026-11-12. **Counsel must confirm:** that it is enforceable for a contractor who may live in any country (pack B-6.3); whether it should sit inside the contractor agreement or beside it; whether, under GDPR Art. 28(3)(b) and 29, the reviewer is acting under our authority (staff) or is a processor needing a DPA; the governing-law clause.

**Who signs it.** The paid native reader of each target language (ADR-24.14), who reviews sampled episodes and performs the language gate's writing check; taggers, who label pseudonymized transcripts for calibration (ADR-24.14); and anyone else given the `reviewer` or `tagger` role. Signed electronically before the account is activated; the signing date is recorded in `staff_accounts.agreement_signed_at`, and an account without it cannot sign in (07 §4.13).

## Confidentiality agreement

Between `[Company legal name]`, `[Address]` ("For one", "we") and `[Reviewer's full name]`, `[Country of residence]` ("you").

**1. Why this exists.** For one makes a personal show for each learner. To check its quality, you will see material about real people: episode scripts and articles written for them, and, if you are a tagger, transcripts of what they said. Names are replaced by codes like `[NAME]`, but the material can still describe a person's life. Learners trust us with it. This agreement is how you help us keep that trust.

**2. What is confidential.** Everything you see through For one's review or research pages, and anything you learn from it about any learner or any person they mention ("learner material"); and For one's unpublished scripts, prompts, character bibles and methods.

**3. What you agree to do.**
1. Use learner material only to do the review or tagging work we ask for.
2. Look at it only through the For one pages we give you access to, on a device only you use, with the screen out of other people's view.
3. Not copy, download, screenshot, photograph, print, forward, record or write down learner material, except the notes the review page asks you to enter.
4. Not paste learner material into any other tool, including any AI assistant, translation service, spreadsheet, chat or email.
5. Not try to find out who a learner is, and not contact or identify anyone in the material, even if you think you recognize them. If you think you do, stop, close the item and tell us; we will give you different items.
6. Not discuss learner material with anyone, except with For one's team for the work itself.
7. Keep your sign-in to yourself. Tell us at once if you think someone else used it, or if any learner material reached anyone it shouldn't have. Tell us within 24 hours at `[security email]`.
8. When your work ends, or when we ask, stop using your account and delete any notes you were allowed to make outside the review page.

**4. What we do.** We show you only what the work needs, with names replaced, one item at a time. Every item you open is logged, and learners can see that a reviewer looked at their material (the role and date, never your name). We tell you if the rules change.

**5. How long.** These duties start when you sign and continue after your work for For one ends, for as long as the material is confidential. Learner material never stops being confidential.

**6. If this is broken.** Breaking this agreement is a serious matter: we may end your access and your work at once, and we may have to tell regulators and the learners affected. We may also take legal action for any loss caused. Nothing here stops you from reporting wrongdoing to a regulator or as the law allows.

**7. Law.** This agreement is governed by `[Governing law]`. `[Counsel: courts, and whether the reviewer's local law requires anything else.]`
