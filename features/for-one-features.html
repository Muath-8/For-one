THE CALL · 5 TO 7 MIN

A real argument, with a host who waits for you

The host takes a side and pushes back. When you get something wrong, she says it back the right way and keeps the conversation going, like a friend would. When a word won't come, you hold one button and say it in your own language.

The goal is 4+ minutes of your own speech; the host talks at most 40% of the time. Recall moments and a 60-second retell make you pull last week's words back out. If you freeze, she gets gentler and simpler. Bad signal turns it into voice notes. Round two: the same argument again, shorter and faster. during beta
EVERY EVENING

You choose tomorrow's episode tonight

The chat ends with three ideas: usually one from your life, one surprise, and at most one that continues a story. Or tell Salma your own idea. If you don't pick, the teacher's choice waits for you.

You own the topic, the format and the cast. The teacher fits the lesson into whatever you choose. One swap a day if the dropped episode doesn't appeal. 2 and 3 a day plans get the other ideas made on demand, the same day.

ANY TIME

Everything the show knows, in Salma's notebook

The teacher is invisible, but nothing is hidden. The notebook shows what it is working on with you, each with one of your own sentences, the words you now use yourself, and every fact it remembers about you with where it came from.

Veto or add to what the teacher works on. It changes the next day. A wrong memory is a bug. Fix it and it is fixed at once. Counts only grow: words you own, hours heard. Nothing resets, nothing has a target.

YOUR DECISION: ANYONE, ANYWHERE

No target country. A language opens when it passes five tests.

Each show is built from one person's life, so nothing is tuned to a market. The real limit is quality per language, so a test decides, not a vendor's list.

HOW IT IS MADE

One teacher, many hands, and only one of them in a hurry

Only the host on the call has to be fast. Everything that needs deep thinking happens the night before or right after, where it can be slow, cheap and checked.

THE PLAN

Pilot by hand, then build, then charge

Each step has a gate. If one fails, find out which belief failed (nobody wants the show, nobody feels the teacher, or the price is wrong) before writing more code.

The bet to watch

Every expert review recommended telling learners their mistakes after the call. You chose invisible teaching. Saying it back the right way is the easiest kind of feedback to miss, so the plan repeats the right form often, makes the call need it, and measures accuracy in every 4-week recording.
