07 · Capacity

The honest arithmetic

Story sizes counted as half a day, one day and two and a half days. Solid is P0, hatched is P1. Two engineers have about 50 working days before 23 December.

About 110 engineer-days of P0 against 50 available. The plan holds only if the architecture's single-engineer cut lists are applied from day one even with two engineers, the P1 set waits for the beta, and the week 1 and 2 vendor tests are Muath's time rather than engineering time. The Show tab, admin pages, Sign in with Apple and the invites page are already accepted as beta-week-one items. If the week 3 exit (pilot under 45 s, first sentence under 5 minutes) is missed, the beta moves to 6 January. This is risk R1.
