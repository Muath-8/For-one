08 · Launch

The launch checklist

Version 1 opens to everyone when every line is green. Muath ticks each one; the agent produces the evidence. The beta gates are now launch health measures, read 4 and 8 weeks after launch with the same targets and stop lines.

  1. Every v1 story passes its acceptance tests on main, and every phase exit check has passed.
  2. The v1 non-functional stories pass: latency, cost tracing, deletion windows, clean logs, AI disclosure and crisis, the laws in lint.
  3. eval-gate is green: law scan, governor replay, editor, and zero disclosures on the injection set.
  4. The self-review is complete: every legal text passed the AI review pass and Muath's approval; DPIA and RoPA done.
  5. Every vendor has its standard DPA accepted and training opt-out recorded; zero retention requested from Anthropic.
  6. An EU and UK representative is appointed if the company sits outside them.
  7. Paddle is live with the three prices, the 7-day trial without a card and the 14-day refund.
  8. The domain is live with email sending.
  9. Production restored once into staging within 4 hours; backups at 30 days with point-in-time recovery.
  10. The launch rehearsal meets the onboarding budgets, with five nights of drops and none missed.
  11. Measured cost per session on production, with a costed path to $0.35; the spend cap and halt tested.
  12. Alerts reach Muath; runbooks R1 to R4 and R12 rehearsed by the agent on staging; the crisis fail-safe tested.
  13. Security checks green: dependency and secret scans in CI, an OWASP ZAP baseline of staging, the AI security review on auth, keys, media URLs, the feed and deletion. No paid pentest at launch.
  14. The English package is shippable; native/ar ships only once Muath has read Salma's Arabic and its blind test passed.
