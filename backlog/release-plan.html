The release plan

The open follows the work. A build of 136.5 engineer-days of Day-1 work from 18 November, two engineers, a paid founding beta from Wednesday 17 March 2027, the gate read on 20 April, and one season after. Story sizes unchanged; the date is computed from them and re-forecast every week.

05 · Cuts and manual substitutes

What a cohort of fifty can live without on day one

The learner-facing core is real on day one: onboarding, the pipeline, the drop and the feed, the call, learner-model writes, the after-session pass, crisis, cost tracing and the spend guard, and voice deletion. What is not is done by hand behind a runbook line with an owner and a limit, and each line is deleted when its story ships. The single-engineer cut list applies from day one, except Terraform, which stays.

07 · Capacity

The honest arithmetic

Story sizes counted as half a day, one day and two and a half days, unchanged since 6 November. Muath's vendor tests, package, counsel and beta operations (7.5 days) are left out. Solid is Day-1, pale is later in the beta, hatched is P1.

136.5 engineer-days of Day-1, plus 15%, is 157.0. Two engineers at five days a week from 18 November have 52 by 23 December; the seven weekdays from 24 December to 1 January count half (7 more); the remaining 98 take 49 weekdays from 4 January and end on Thursday 11 March. Friday 12 March closes the build with a 16.5% buffer, the freeze runs from 15 March 12:00, and the open is Wednesday 17 March.
